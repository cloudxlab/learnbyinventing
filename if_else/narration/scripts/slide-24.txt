# Part 6: Do Two Rectangles Intersect? (2D)
# ---
Last time, you checked whether a single point sits inside a rectangle by looking at one direction at a time. Now let's go one step further. Imagine two rectangles floating on a plane. How would you tell if they overlap? Before you write any code, try thinking about the x-direction and y-direction separately. You've already got the tools for this.
