Think of a bag with red and blue marbles. Your formula needs three things: zero when everything is one class, higher as the mix gets more even, and highest at a perfect fifty-fifty split. There's no single right answer, so try a formula, test it against those three rules, and tweak it. Give it a real try before you peek at the example.
