# A Three-Way Branch / only gives you two outcomes. // gives you more. Write a function that takes a die result (1 through 6) and returns: if is 1 or 2 if is 3 or 4 if is 5 or 6 Tasks: Implement it using //. Roll the die 20 times with and classify each result. Print both the roll and its class.
# ---
Regular if-else gives you two outcomes: this or that. But what if you need three? Here's a puzzle. Take a die roll, one through six, and sort it into three groups: low, middle, and high. Before you reach for a chain of conditions, try integer division. It might do the work for you. Give it a go, then roll twenty times to test it.
