Now we're moving from one segment to two. Here's a trick: instead of figuring out when they overlap, think about when they're completely apart. Remember, you can't assume either segment is written in order, so sort each one first. Then ask yourself, when is the first one fully to the left of the second? And when is it fully to the right? Write those down, then flip the logic to get "touching or overlapping." Give it a try.
