Here's a neat shortcut for this one. We want to know whether point P is closer to A or to B. You might think we need real distances, square roots and all. But we don't. Whichever distance is smaller, its square is smaller too, so we can skip the square root completely. Grab a pencil, work out both squared distances, and see which point wins.
