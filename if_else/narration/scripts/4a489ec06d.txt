# Part 2: Comparing Two Distances — Closer to A or B?

Now that we can handle a single distance, let's make it a bit more interesting. Imagine you're standing somewhere, and there are two spots, A and B. Which one are you closer to? Before we look at any code, think about what you'd need to compare, and what decision you'd have to make once you know.
