# ON SCREEN:
# Part 5: Is a Point Inside a Rectangle? (2D)
# ---
We've been working with one dimension, where a point sits on a line and a range has a left end and a right end. Now let's step up to two dimensions. Picture a rectangle on a flat surface, and a point somewhere near it. How would you decide whether that point is inside? Think about what you already figured out for the line. It might help more than you expect.
