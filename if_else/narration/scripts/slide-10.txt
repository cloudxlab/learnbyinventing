# Part 3: Is a Point Between Two Others? (1D)
# ---
Now let's take the same branching ideas into a new puzzle. Imagine three numbers on a number line, and you want to know whether one of them sits between the other two. It sounds simple, but there's a small catch: you don't know which of the other two is bigger. Before you code anything, grab a pencil and think about how you'd check it.
