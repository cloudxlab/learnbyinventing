# Part 1: Branching with Randomness
# ---
Welcome to Part 1! We're starting with something fun: randomness. Imagine your program flipping a coin, and then doing different things depending on how it lands. Heads, one thing happens. Tails, something else. That's branching, and it's the idea we'll invent together. Let's see how a program can make a choice.
