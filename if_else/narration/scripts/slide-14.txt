# ON SCREEN:
# Part 4: Do Two Segments Touch or Overlap? (1D)
# ---
Now we're moving from single numbers to something richer: segments on a line. Picture two stretches of road, each with a start and an end. Do they touch? Do they overlap? It sounds simple, but there are a surprising number of ways two segments can sit next to each other. Before we write any code, try sketching a few arrangements on paper. What do you notice?
