# ON SCREEN:
# can_place The checker. Write — may digit legally go into cell ? If already appears anywhere in that row — . If already appears anywhere in that column — . If already appears in the cell's 3×3 block — . Otherwise .
# ---
Now it's time to build the checker. Given a digit and a cell, can_place answers one question: is it legal to put that digit there? Think about the three rules of Sudoku. The digit can't already be in the row, the column, or the little three-by-three block. If none of those catch it, it's fine. Try writing it yourself first.
