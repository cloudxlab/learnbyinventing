# ON SCREEN:
# Part 3: Permutations
# ---
Part 3 is all about permutations. Up to now, we've been listing out possibilities one way or another, but this time there's a twist. We're going to arrange things in different orders, where each item can be used only once. Before we look at any code, grab a pencil and think about this. If you had three friends to line up for a photo, how many different orders could you make?
