Now let's flip the view. An n-digit number is just a first digit followed by a shorter number with one fewer digit. So what's the simplest case? Zero digits, where there's exactly one string: the empty one. From there, prepend each digit to everything you already have. Try writing it yourself, and make sure it matches your loop version. Then ask: how many strings come out? You've seen that formula before.
