# handshakes Write that returns the list of all unique pairs as tuples. A pair should appear once: if is in the list, must not be. [code block] Think: for people, how many handshakes will there be? Test your formula with the last assertion.
# ---
Here's a small puzzle to start with. Imagine a room full of people, and everyone shakes hands with everyone else exactly once. Your job is to write a function that lists every unique pair. If Alice and Bob is in the list, Bob and Alice shouldn't be.

Before you code, try a quick pencil-and-paper question. If there are a handful of people, how many handshakes would that be? See if you can spot a pattern, then use it to check your answer against the last assertion. Give it a go yourself first.
