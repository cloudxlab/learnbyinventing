Now we're moving into a new idea: listing every possible combination, one by one. Imagine the odometer in a car, those little spinning number wheels. Each wheel counts up, and when the rightmost one hits its top digit, it rolls back to zero and nudges the next wheel over by one. Hold onto that picture, because we're going to turn it into code.
