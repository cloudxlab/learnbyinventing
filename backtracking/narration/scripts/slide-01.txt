# Part 1: Warm-Up — Enumerating Pairs A room of people all shake hands, each pair exactly once.
# ---
Let's start with something you've probably seen at a party. Imagine a room full of people, and everyone shakes hands with everyone else, but each pair only shakes once. Before we write any code, think about how you'd keep track of who's already met. That question is our warm-up, so give it a try yourself.
