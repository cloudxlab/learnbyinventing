Now we're moving to a new part, and it's all about checking a state. Here's the secret of exhaustive search: it lives or dies by its checker, that little function that looks at a possibility and says "fine" or "broken." Before we tackle Sudoku, let's warm up with brackets. Think about what makes a bunch of brackets valid.
