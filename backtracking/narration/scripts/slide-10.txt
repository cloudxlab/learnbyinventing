# Quick Check 3.1 — Permutation Count How many permutations does a list of 4 distinct elements have? A. 4 B. 16 C. 24 D. 256
# ---
Time for a quick check. You've been building permutations step by step, so you've got a feel for how fast the options multiply. Before you pick an answer, think about the choices you had for the first position, then the second, then the third. How do those choices shrink as you go? Work it out on paper, then choose.
