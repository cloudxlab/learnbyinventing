Here's a fun one: checking whether a string of brackets is valid. Every opener has to be closed by the matching closer, and in the right order. So an open round bracket followed by a close square bracket? That's not valid.

Before you write any code, try a few strings on paper. As you scan left to right, what do you need to remember? Think about which openers are still waiting for their closers, and which one gets closed first. What kind of container gives you the most recent item first? Give it a go yourself.
