# Backtracking — Exhaustive Search, Done Right Some problems have no formula. Nobody can compute the solution of a Sudoku puzzle — you have to try possibilities . The art is in trying them systematically: Enumerate — walk through every candidate, missing none, repeating none. Check — recognize a valid (or doomed) state quickly. Backtrack — when a choice leads to a dead end, undo it and try the next one. You already met this idea once: the wildcard in the Pattern Matching chapter tried both branches and gave up on the ones that failed. In this chapter you build the technique up from tiny exercises until you can solve any Sudoku board. Prerequisite: the Recursion chapter. Navigate: press or Space to advance | to go back
