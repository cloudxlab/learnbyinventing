# Part 5: Sudoku The main event. A Sudoku board is a 9×9 grid; every row , every column , and every 3×3 block must contain the digits 1–9 exactly once. The puzzle gives you some digits (the clues ); you fill the rest. We represent the board as a list of 9 lists, with meaning empty .

Welcome to the main event: Sudoku. You've probably solved one of these on paper, so you know the rules. Every row, every column, and every three-by-three block must hold the digits one through nine, each exactly once. We'll store the board as a list of nine lists, with zero meaning empty.
