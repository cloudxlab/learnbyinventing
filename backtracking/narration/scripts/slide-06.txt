Now let's look at the same problem from a recursive angle. An n-digit number is just a first digit followed by a shorter number, one digit fewer. So what's the base case? Think about it: there's exactly one string of length zero, the empty one. Then for each digit, stick it on the front of every shorter string. Try writing it yourself, and check that it matches your first version. Then ask: how many strings come back? You've actually seen this counting idea in our very first chapter.
