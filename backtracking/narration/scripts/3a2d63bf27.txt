# ---
Here's a thought to sit with: this puzzle has fifty-one empty cells. If we tried every possible combination blindly, that's more boards than there are atoms in a small galaxy. The sun would burn out first. And yet your solver finishes in under a second. So what's going on? The secret is pruning. The moment a digit breaks a rule, we drop that entire branch right there, so the giant tree almost never gets built. Enumeration makes sure we don't miss anything, and checking makes it fast.
