# Exercise: can_place. Write can_place(i, row, col, board) checking if digit i can legally go into (row, col). Three constraints: row, column, and 3x3 block. Before coding: how do you find the top-left corner of the block? Hints and solution included.

Now the checker, which is the heart of the Sudoku solver. Write can_place to decide whether a digit may legally go into a given cell. You need to check three constraints: the row, the column, and the three-by-three block. Before you code, figure out how to find the top-left corner of the block that contains your cell. That's the trickiest part.
