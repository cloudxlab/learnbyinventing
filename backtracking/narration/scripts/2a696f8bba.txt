# Part 2: Enumerating Everything — The Odometer Print all numbers of length where each digit runs from to — that is, all n-digit numbers in base m. (No allowed — you are inventing it.) First idea: a car odometer. To go from to the rightmost wheel rolls over to and carries into the next wheel.
# ---
Now we're moving into enumeration: printing every possible number of a given length, where each digit can take any value in a range. Think of a car odometer. Each wheel ticks up, and when the rightmost one hits its top, it rolls back to zero and nudges the next wheel over. Before we go further, try to picture that rollover. What exactly happens to the wheels, step by step? That pattern is what you'll turn into code, with no shortcuts allowed.
