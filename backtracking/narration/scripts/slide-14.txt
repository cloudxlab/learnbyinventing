# ON SCREEN:
# can_place The checker. Write — may digit legally go into cell ? If already appears anywhere in that row — . If already appears anywhere in that column — . If already appears in the cell's 3×3 block — . Otherwise .
# ---
Here's the checker. Before you write anything, think about the rules like a Sudoku player would. A digit can't repeat in its row, it can't repeat in its column, and it can't repeat in its little three-by-three block. If any of those three checks fails, the answer is no. Otherwise, yes.

The tricky part is finding which block a cell belongs to, so work that out on paper first. Then give it a try yourself before moving on.
