# Quick Check 5.1 — Why Backtracking Prunes A 9×9 Sudoku has 81 cells. If you tried every possible assignment of digits 1–9 to all empty cells (brute force without checking constraints), approximately how many combinations would that be for a puzzle with 50 empty cells? A. 50 × 9 = 450 B. 9^50 — an astronomically large number C. 50! (50 factorial) D. 81 × 9 = 729
# ---
Time for a quick check, and this one's about why backtracking is such a big deal. Picture a Sudoku with fifty empty cells. If you tried every possible way to fill them with digits one through nine, ignoring the rules completely, roughly how many combinations would that be? Think about how many choices each cell gives you, and what happens when you stack fifty of those together. Pick your answer before we move on.
