Welcome to the main event: Sudoku! You've probably seen these puzzles before. It's a nine by nine grid, and every row, every column, and every three by three block has to hold the digits one through nine exactly once. The puzzle gives you a few clues, and you fill in the rest. We'll store the board as a list of nine lists, with zero meaning an empty cell. Everything you've built so far is about to come together.
