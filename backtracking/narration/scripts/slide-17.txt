# Think — Why Is This Fast? The puzzle has 51 empty cells. Blind enumeration would try up to 9⁵¹ ≈ 10⁴⁸ boards — the sun would burn out first. Your solver finishes in well under a second. The difference is pruning : kills a bad branch at its first wrong digit, so the enormous tree of possibilities almost entirely never gets built. Enumeration provides completeness; checking provides the speed.
