Welcome to Backtracking. Some problems have no shortcut formula. You just have to try things. But the art is in trying them systematically: enumerate every possibility, check whether it works, and when it doesn't, undo your choice and try the next one. By the end of this chapter, you'll solve a Sudoku puzzle from scratch.
