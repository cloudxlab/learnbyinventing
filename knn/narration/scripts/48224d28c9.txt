Look at everything you've built over this chapter. Every piece has a real name that experts use. Your distance function is Euclidean distance. Your nearest-neighbor finder is 1-NN, and extending it to a vote among several neighbors gave you K-NN with plurality voting. Testing by leaving each point out was leave-one-out cross-validation. And swapping in a different distance, the taxicab one, showed that the whole idea works with any way of measuring closeness.
