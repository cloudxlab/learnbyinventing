# Quick Check: You have 3 nearest neighbors with labels . What does majority vote predict? A. apple (2 votes to 1) B. orange (it s the nearest) C. unknown (can t decide) D. both equally
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: A. Apple wins 2 1. Each neighbor gets one vote regardless of its distance.
# ---
Yes, A! Apple wins two to one. In plain majority voting, every neighbor gets exactly one vote, no matter how close or far it is. So even though the orange might be the nearest, it's still outvoted.
