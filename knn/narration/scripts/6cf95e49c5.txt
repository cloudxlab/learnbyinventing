# What happens as [math] changes? [math] Behavior 1 Copies the nearest label exactly memorizes noise 3 7 Smooths out outliers usually a sweet spot [math] Always predicts the most common class ignores the data
# ---
Here's the big question: what happens as K changes? When K is one, the model copies the nearest label exactly, which means it memorizes noise, including any weird outliers. Somewhere in the middle, like three or seven, you smooth those outliers out, and that's usually the sweet spot. And at the far end, it just predicts the most common class every time and ignores your data completely. Take a moment to think about why.
