So we've got a working classifier, but here's the real question: how do we know if it's any good? Think about it. We already have a dataset where every point comes with its true label. So here's a neat trick: take each point, hide its label, and predict it using all the *other* points. Then check whether we got it right. Give writing that a try yourself.
