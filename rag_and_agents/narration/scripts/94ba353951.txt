Nice work. What you just built is the skeleton of RAG: before the model ever sees your question, a retrieval step picks out the relevant context and hands over only that. That idea is the heart of the whole approach.

But there's a catch. Matching keywords only works when the question and the document happen to share the same words. So we need a way to measure meaning, not just words. Let's figure out how.
