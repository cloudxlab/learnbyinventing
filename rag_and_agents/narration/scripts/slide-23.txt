# What you invented: the agentic loop — the core architecture of every AI agent, from simple assistants to autonomous research systems. This is also known as the ReAct pattern (Reasoning + Acting).

You just invented the agentic loop. It's the core design behind every AI agent, from a simple assistant to a full autonomous research system. The model thinks about what to do, takes an action, looks at what came back, and then decides what to do next, over and over, until the job is done. Experts call this the ReAct pattern, short for Reasoning plus Acting. Look at that: you didn't memorize it, you built it.
