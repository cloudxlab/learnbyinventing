Here's a thought: an agent sounds fancy, but I think it's just a loop. Before you write any code, grab a pencil and paper and draw it as a flowchart.

Start with a box that sends the question and the conversation so far to the model. Then draw two branches, one for each thing the model might say back. What does your program do in each case?

And the big question: when does the loop stop? Give it a real try first.
