You just invented the agentic loop. This is the core architecture behind every AI agent you'll hear about, and it's also called the ReAct pattern, short for reasoning and acting. Here's the surprising part: the model itself remembers nothing between calls. All the magic comes from the system around it, the message history, the tool dispatcher, and the memory store. Together, they let it reason over many steps and take real actions.
