You just invented semantic search. This is the retrieval backbone of nearly every production RAG system. Here's the whole idea: embeddings turn meaning into numbers, and the dot product tells you how similar two meanings are. That's why "hotel," "accommodation," and "lodging" all land close together, even though they share almost no letters. Your code never matched words. It matched meaning.
