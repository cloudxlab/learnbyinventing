Look at what you just built. You embed every document once, store those vectors, and then at question time you only have to embed the question itself. That's the big win: the expensive work happens up front, and searching becomes quick.

This idea has a name: a vector index. It's the core of tools like Pinecone, Weaviate, and pgvector. You invented it yourself.
