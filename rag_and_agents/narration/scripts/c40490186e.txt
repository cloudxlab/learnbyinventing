Here are five reflection questions. First, why doesn't stuffing all documents into the prompt work, and what's the key idea behind retrieval? Second, how do embeddings fix the problem that keyword matching can't handle? Could you explain embeddings to a friend? Third, why is the confidence gate important, and what goes wrong without it? Fourth, what makes the agent loop different from a single tool call? And fifth, if you had to explain RAG in one sentence, what would you say? Take your time with these. There's real value in putting your understanding into words.
