# Part 2: The Search Problem The central question: how do you find a document that means the same thing, even when it uses different words?
# ---
Now we get to the heart of it: the search problem. Imagine someone asks about "getting my money back," but the document you need says "refund policy." Different words, same meaning. A plain keyword match would miss it completely. So here's the question to hold onto: how could a program find a document that means the same thing, even when the words don't match?
