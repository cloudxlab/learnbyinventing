# What you invented: document chunking with overlap — a standard preprocessing step in all production RAG systems. The overlap prevents relevant sentences from being split across chunk boundaries.
# ---
You just built something real. Splitting documents into chunks with a little overlap is a standard step in production RAG systems. Here's why it matters: without overlap, a sentence that straddles a boundary gets cut in half, and neither chunk makes sense on its own. Overlap keeps those ideas together, so search can actually find them.
