# Part 4: Building a Vector Index

Welcome to Part 4. So far, you've seen how to turn text into numbers, and how to measure whether two pieces of text are close in meaning. Now comes a practical question: what happens when you have thousands of documents instead of a handful? In this part, you'll build a vector index, a way to store and search all those numbers quickly. Let's dive in.
