# ON SCREEN:
# Part 4: The Single-Step Limit The central question: what happens when answering a question requires doing something — not just knowing something?
# ---
So far, our assistant can look things up and answer from what it finds. But now let's push it. What if a question needs the assistant to actually do something, not just know something? Maybe check a live number, run a calculation, or take an action. Before we move on, think about where a single lookup-then-answer step might break down.
