Congratulations, you've just built a complete RAG pipeline! You retrieve the relevant chunks, check how confident the match is, and only then ask the model to answer from what you found. That confidence gate is a small idea, but it's a real production pattern. It stops the model from making things up when retrieval comes back empty. What you invented has a name: Retrieval-Augmented Generation, the go-to way to ground language models in private or recent knowledge.
