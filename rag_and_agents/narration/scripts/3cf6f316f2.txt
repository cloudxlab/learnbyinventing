You just built something real: a vector index. It's the same core idea behind tools like Pinecone, Weaviate, and pgvector. Here's the trick that makes it fast. You embed your documents once, up front, during indexing. Then at query time, you only embed the question. That's one embedding, not thousands, and that's why search stays quick.
