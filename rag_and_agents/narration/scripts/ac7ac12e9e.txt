Here's a puzzle about documents that are too long. Imagine you embed a whole ten-page PDF as a single vector. That one vector has to cover every topic on every page, so it ends up a blurry average of everything. Now go the other way and embed each sentence on its own. Every piece loses the context around it. So there's a Goldilocks size somewhere in the middle. Before you look at the hint, think about what "just right" might look like. Then try writing the loop yourself.
