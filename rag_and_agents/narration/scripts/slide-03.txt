You've just felt the core problem: a language model's knowledge is frozen at the moment it was trained. It can't know what happened yesterday, and it can't see your company's private data. Your keyword check was a clever patch, but it's still a patch. The real fix is to hand the model the right document at the moment you ask the question. And look what you just invented: the idea that knowledge should come from outside the model, not locked inside it. That's the heart of retrieval-augmented generation.
