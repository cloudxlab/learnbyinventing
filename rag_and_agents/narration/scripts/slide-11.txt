# Part 3: Inventing RAG The central question: how do you combine retrieval with generation to get answers that are grounded in your documents?
# ---
Here's the big question for this part: how do we combine looking things up with actually writing an answer, so the response is grounded in your own documents? We've seen that search can find the right text, and that a model can write fluent sentences. Now it's time to bring those two together. Before we move on, think about it. How would you wire them up yourself?
