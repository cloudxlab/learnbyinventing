Congratulations, you've just built a complete RAG pipeline! Think about what you put together: you retrieve the most relevant chunks, check how confident the search was, and only then generate an answer grounded in what you found.

That confidence gate is a small idea with a big payoff. When retrieval comes up empty, the model admits it instead of making something up. Real production systems rely on exactly this.

And the name for what you invented? Retrieval-Augmented Generation, the go-to way to ground language models in private or recent knowledge.
