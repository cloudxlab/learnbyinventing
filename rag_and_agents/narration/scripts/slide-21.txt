# Part 5: Inventing Agents The central question: how do you give a model the ability to act repeatedly until a task is complete?
# ---
Welcome to our last big part: inventing agents. So far, our model can look things up and use tools, but only once per question. Real tasks aren't like that. They take several steps, and what you need next depends on what you just found. So here's the question to chew on: how would you let a model keep acting, again and again, until the job is actually done? Give it some thought before we go on.
