Some questions need more than one lookup. Think about asking "who manages the person who wrote this report?" One tool call can't answer that, so you need a loop. The model calls a tool, reads the result, and decides whether it needs another. That loop is what we call an agent. You've just invented the bridge between a model's words and real actions.
