# Part 5: Inventing Agents The central question: how do you give a model the ability to act repeatedly until a task is complete?
# ---
So far, your model can search for knowledge and even use a tool once. But what happens when a task needs several steps, where each step depends on what the last one found? One call isn't enough. Here's the question for this part: how would you let a model keep acting, again and again, until the job is actually done? Take a moment and think about it.
