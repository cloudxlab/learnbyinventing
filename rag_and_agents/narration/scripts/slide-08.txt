You just used embeddings, which turn the meaning of a piece of text into a list of numbers. And here's the neat part: when you take the dot product of two of those lists, you get a score for how close in meaning the two texts are. That's the engine inside nearly every modern search system. So without realizing it, you've invented semantic search, the retrieval backbone of real RAG systems.
