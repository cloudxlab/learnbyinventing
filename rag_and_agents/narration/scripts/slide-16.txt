# ON SCREEN:
# Part 4: The Single-Step Limit The central question: what happens when answering a question requires doing something — not just knowing something?
# ---
We've seen how retrieval lets a model look things up before it answers. That solves a knowledge problem. But here's a new kind of question: what if answering means actually doing something? Booking a flight, running a calculation, updating a record. Knowing isn't enough anymore. Take a moment and think about where a single lookup-then-answer step would break down.
