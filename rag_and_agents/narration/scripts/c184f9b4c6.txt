You've just felt the core problem: a language model's knowledge is frozen at the moment it was trained. It can't know what happened yesterday, and it can't see your company's private files. Your keyword check was a clever patch, but not a real fix. The real fix is handing the model the right document at the moment you ask. That's the seed of retrieval-augmented generation: knowledge comes from outside the model, not from inside it.
