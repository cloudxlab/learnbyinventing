# What You Invented
# ---
You just invented RAG and agents, and you did it one small problem at a time. You started with a model that couldn't know your private data. Then you taught it to search, to pull in the right context, and to use tools. Finally you gave it a loop, so it could decide what to do next on its own. Take a moment to see how those pieces fit together. You built every one of them yourself.
