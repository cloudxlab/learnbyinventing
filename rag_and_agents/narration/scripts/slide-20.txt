# ---
A single tool call is great for simple questions, but real questions are messier. Sometimes the answer to one tool call tells you that you need a second one, and maybe a third. So what do you need? A loop. The model calls a tool, looks at the result, and decides whether it needs another. That loop is what we call an agent. And you just invented it: the bridge between a model's words and real actions in the world.
