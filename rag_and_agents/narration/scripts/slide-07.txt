Welcome to Part 2. Now we get to the heart of the problem: search. Imagine you have a pile of documents, and someone asks a question using completely different words than the ones in your answer. A plain keyword search would miss it. So here's the question we'll chase together: how do you find a document that means the same thing, even when the words don't match? Let's start inventing.
