Look at what you just built. You retrieve the most relevant chunks, check your confidence before answering, and only then generate a grounded response. That's Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and it's the standard way to give a language model private or recent knowledge. Notice the confidence gate: it's what stops the model from making things up when retrieval comes up empty. And overlapping chunks kept important sentences from getting cut in half.
