# ON SCREEN:
# The key idea: What if you could turn the meaning of a sentence into a list of numbers ?
# ---
Here's a question to chew on: how would a computer ever "understand" what a sentence means? Computers are great with numbers, so what if we could turn the meaning of a sentence into a list of numbers? Pause for a second and imagine it. Two sentences that mean similar things would end up with similar lists. That's the key idea we're going to build toward.
