# You walk east 3 blocks, then north 4 blocks. Your position is the vector .

You walk east three blocks, then north four. Where did you end up? Notice we can describe that spot with just two numbers: how far east, and how far north. Bundle those two numbers together, and you've got a position. That bundle is what we'll call a vector. Pretty simple, right? Let's see what else it can do.
