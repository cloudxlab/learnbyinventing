# [Fragment 0]: Ratings are vectors. Cosine similarity tells you how aligned two people s tastes are regardless of whether one person rates everything higher than the other.
# ---
Here's the big idea: a movie rating list is just a vector, one number per movie. Cosine similarity compares the direction of two vectors, not their size. So it can tell when two friends have the same taste even if one of them hands out generous fives and the other is a tougher grader.
