# ON SCREEN:
# The dot product is affected by vector lengths. A vector twice as long gives a dot product twice as big, even if the direction is the same.
# ---
Here's something that might surprise you. The dot product doesn't only care about direction. It also cares about how long the vectors are. Take a vector and stretch it to twice its length, keeping it pointing the exact same way, and the dot product doubles. Same direction, bigger number. Hold onto that. It's going to matter in a moment.
