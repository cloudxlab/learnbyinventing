Sometimes the world just won't cooperate. Here's a factory with three measurements, and when we try to draw a straight line through them, every attempt misses at least one point. Real measurements are noisy like that, so perfect solving breaks down. But we don't have to give up. We can settle for the line that's least wrong. The big question is how to measure "wrong," and that's your next challenge.
