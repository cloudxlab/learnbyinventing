Here's the final step. You've fitted a line through two points, and you've solved equations for exact data. Now let's handle messy, real-world data, where no single line hits every point.

Your job is to build a function that takes any list of pairs and computes the handful of sums you need. From those sums you'll set up the two normal equations and solve them for the best line.

Try writing it yourself before you peek at anything. Then test it on perfectly linear data. It should give you back the exact line, because exact fitting is just a special case of least squares.
