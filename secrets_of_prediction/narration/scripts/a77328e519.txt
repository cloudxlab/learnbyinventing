# When you apply one transformation and then another, you can combine them into a single matrix. That s matrix multiplication .
# ---
Here's a neat thing. Suppose you rotate a point, and then stretch it. That's two separate transformations, two separate matrices. But what if you wanted to do both in one go? It turns out you can squash them into a single matrix that does the exact same job. That's what we call matrix multiplication, and you're about to invent it.
