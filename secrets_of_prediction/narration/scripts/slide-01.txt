# Part 1: Vectors --- the Arithmetic of Prediction A fitted model is just a list of coefficients --- a vector . Evaluating it at a point is a dot product . Build that toolkit first.

Welcome to Part One. Here's a big idea to start with: when you fit a model to data, what you end up with is just a list of numbers, the coefficients. That list has a name, a vector. And when you use the model to make a prediction, you're combining that list with your inputs in one simple, repeatable way. Before we go further, let's build the small toolkit that makes this possible.
