Here's a way to compare two vectors that doesn't depend on how long they are. Shrink each one to a unit vector, then take the dot product. What comes out is the cosine of the angle between them. It's one when they point the same way, zero when they're perpendicular, and negative one when they're opposite. This one number is how modern systems compare documents, faces, and embeddings. Give it a go yourself, then check it against the examples.
