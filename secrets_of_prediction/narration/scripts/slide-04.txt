Here's a question: how do you tell whether two vectors point the same way, without caring how long they are? We already know the trick. Turn each one into a unit vector, then take their dot product. What comes out is the cosine of the angle between them. Same direction, perpendicular, opposite: each gives a number you can work out. Try writing it yourself, then check it against the examples.
