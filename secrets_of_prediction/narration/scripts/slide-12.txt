Traffic at a junction has been measured three times, and the growth isn't a straight line. Look at the gaps: six, then eight. So a line won't cut it. Let's try a curve instead, a quadratic with three unknown numbers. Each measurement gives you one equation, so you'll have three equations for three unknowns. Build them, solve them, then predict the next hour. Give it a go yourself first.
