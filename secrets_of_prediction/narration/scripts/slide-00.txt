Welcome! Let's start with a little puzzle from the market. You buy three apples and the trip costs six. Next week, five apples cost eight. Can you figure out what ten apples will cost? Try writing the story as equations, and see if you can find the price of one apple and the parking fee. This is the oldest prediction machine there is: turn what you've observed into equations, solve them, and extrapolate. By the end of this chapter, you'll rebuild the whole thing yourself.
