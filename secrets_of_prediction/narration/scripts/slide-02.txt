# Part 1: Vectors --- the Arithmetic of Prediction A fitted model is just a list of coefficients --- a vector . Evaluating it at a point is a dot product . Build that toolkit first.
# ---
Welcome to Part 1. Here's a neat idea: when a model is fitted, everything it learned boils down to a simple list of numbers, which we call a vector. Making a prediction means combining that list with your inputs in one specific way, called a dot product. Let's build that toolkit first, piece by piece.
