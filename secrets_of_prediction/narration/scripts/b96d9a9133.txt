You just derived Ordinary Least Squares, a closed-form answer that lands on the best fit in one jump. So why does the next chapter exist? Two cracks show up. First, solving gets painfully slow as the problem grows, just like you estimated back in Part 2. Second, setting the derivative to zero only works if you can write down a formula for that derivative, and complicated models rarely give you one. Gradient descent fixes both by walking toward the minimum instead of jumping straight to it.
