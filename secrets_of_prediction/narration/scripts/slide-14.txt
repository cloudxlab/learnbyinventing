Here's a puzzle from a factory. Three measurements, and we want to predict the fourth. Try drawing a line through the first two points, and it misses the third. Try the first and third, and now the second one is off. No single line passes through all three.

That's what real, noisy data looks like, and perfect solving just broke. So here's the new plan: accept an imperfect line, but pick the one that's least wrong. The big question is, how do you actually measure "wrong"? That's your challenge next, so give it a real try.
