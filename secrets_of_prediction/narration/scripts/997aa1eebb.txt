# Predicting one sample at a time works, but there s a faster way. If the dataset is a matrix and the weights are a vector

Predicting one sample at a time works, but you've probably noticed it's a bit clunky. You'd need a loop, and that gets slow when you've got thousands of rows. Here's a thought: what if we stored the whole dataset as one grid of numbers, and the weights as a single list? Take a moment and think about what that could let us do.
