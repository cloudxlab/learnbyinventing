# ON SCREEN:
# Rotation matrices are special they preserve shape and size. But what does an arbitrary matrix do?
# ---
We just saw that rotation matrices are special. They move every point, but they keep shapes and sizes exactly the same. Nothing gets stretched or squashed. So here's a question worth sitting with: what about a matrix that isn't a rotation? What do you think an arbitrary matrix does to a shape? Take a moment and make a guess before we go on.
