# You walked east 3, north 4. How far are you from where you started?
# [Fragment 0]: Pythagoras: [math]. That s the length (or magnitude ) of the vector . For any number of dimensions: [math].

That's the Pythagorean theorem at work, and the distance you walked is what we call the length, or magnitude, of the vector. Here it comes out to exactly five steps. The same idea works in any number of dimensions: square each part, add them up, and take the square root.
