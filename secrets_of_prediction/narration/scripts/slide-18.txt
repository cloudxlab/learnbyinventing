Welcome to the bonus round! So far, you've used matrices to hold data, like trips and prices. But a matrix has a second life: it can be a machine that moves points. Every video game and 3D animation runs on this idea. Keep your points as rows, then multiply the whole stack by one small matrix, and every point moves at once. You already have the code for it, so there's nothing new to write.
