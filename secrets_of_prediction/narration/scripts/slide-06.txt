# Cosine Similarity Meaning Two vectors have a cosine similarity of 0. What does this tell you about their relationship? A. They are identical B. They point in exactly opposite directions C. They are perpendicular (at 90 degrees) --- knowing one tells you nothing about the other D. One of the vectors is all zeros
# ---
Here's a quick check on what cosine similarity really means. Say two vectors give you a cosine similarity of zero. Before you look at the options, picture the angle between them. What would that angle have to be for the cosine to come out as zero?

Think about what each choice says about direction. Then pick the one you believe, and we'll see how you did.
