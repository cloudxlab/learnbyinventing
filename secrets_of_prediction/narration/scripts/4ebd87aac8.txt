# Now the big one: how do we measure whether two vectors point in similar directions ?
# ---
Now for the big question. We've been working with vectors, and now we want a way to measure whether two of them point in similar directions. Think about two arrows starting from the same spot. When would you say they're pointing the same way, and when would they be pointing apart? Take a moment and try inventing a number that captures that.
