# ON SCREEN:
# You walked east 3, north 4. How far are you from where you started?
# ---
Here's a quick puzzle before we go on. Picture yourself walking three steps east, then four steps north. You've made two separate moves, but I'm curious about something simpler: if you drew a straight line from where you started to where you ended up, how long would it be?

Try figuring it out yourself first. Sketch the path on paper, and see if you spot a shape hiding in it. Take your time, then let's check your answer together.
