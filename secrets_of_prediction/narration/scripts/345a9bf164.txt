# ON SCREEN:
# Now the big one: how do we measure whether two vectors point in similar directions ?
# [Fragment 1]: Situation Dot product Same direction Large positive Perpendicular (90 ) Zero Opposite direction Large negative
# ---
Here's the pattern: when two vectors point the same way, the dot product comes out large and positive. At a right angle it's exactly zero, and when they point opposite ways it goes large and negative. So one number tells you how aligned two directions are.
