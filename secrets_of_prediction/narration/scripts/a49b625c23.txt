Here's a challenge: you've got a candidate line and a bunch of data points. For each point, the line makes a prediction, and the error is how far off that prediction is. There's a catch, though. Some errors are positive and some are negative, so if you just add them up, they cancel out and a terrible line can look perfect. Before you code anything, think about what simple operation turns every error into a positive number. Then think about how to combine them into one score for "total wrongness." Give it a shot yourself, then write total_error.
