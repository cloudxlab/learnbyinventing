Your next exercise is to write a function called fit. It takes two points and returns a tuple: the slope and the intercept of the line that passes through both. You've already built the pieces, so try it on your own first.

Before you code, think about this. What happens if the two points have the same x value? The line would be vertical, and the slope would mean dividing by zero. You don't need to fix that. Just notice it, and let Python do whatever it naturally does.
