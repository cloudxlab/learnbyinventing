Here's a classic one: the Pythagorean theorem. If you know the two shorter sides of a right triangle, you can find the longest side, the hypotenuse.

Before you write any code, grab a pencil and work it out by hand. Take the two leg lengths you've been given, and figure out what the hypotenuse should be. Then you'll know what your function needs to return.
