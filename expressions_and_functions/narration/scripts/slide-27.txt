# Part 5: Functions That Take Other Functions — The Derivative
# ---
Up to now, our functions have taken numbers and given back numbers. Here's a twist: what if a function could take another function as its input? That's the big idea of this part, and it's exactly how we'll build the derivative. Before we move on, think about it. If you wanted to measure how steeply a function is climbing at one spot, what would you do?
