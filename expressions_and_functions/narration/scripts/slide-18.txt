# Distance Metric Intuition A delivery drone flies in a straight line from point A to point B, but a delivery truck must follow streets laid out in a grid. Which distance formula should you use to estimate each vehicle's travel distance? A. Euclidean for both B. Manhattan for both C. Euclidean for the drone, Manhattan for the truck D. Manhattan for the drone, Euclidean for the truck
# ---
Here's a puzzle that sounds simple, but it's really about how you measure distance. Picture a delivery drone flying straight from point A to point B. Now picture a truck that has to follow a grid of streets. Think about how each one actually moves, and which formula you've built that matches. Give it a try before you look at the options.
