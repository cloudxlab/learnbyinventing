The decoder needs to go the other direction: from integer IDs back to emails. So you need to flip the mapping around. If you did this in the Dictionaries chapter, try rebuilding it from memory. Also think about why this flip is safe. What property of the mapping guarantees that no information gets lost when you invert it?
