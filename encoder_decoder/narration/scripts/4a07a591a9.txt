# Part 6: What You Just Invented The same encode, black box, decode shape appears everywhere in computing...
# ---
Take a step back and look at the big picture. The pattern you just built, encode, process, decode, is one of the most common patterns in all of computing. Converting text to numbers for a model, compressing data before sending it over a network, even your Huffman coder from the information theory chapter. The black box in the middle changes, but the wrapper pattern stays the same.
