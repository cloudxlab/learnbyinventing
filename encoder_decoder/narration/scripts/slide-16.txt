# ---
You've just built the whole pipeline, and here's the big idea: it's a pattern you'll see everywhere. Whenever you one-hot-encode categories for a model, tokenize text for an LLM, or compress data before sending it across a network, it's the same shape. Encode, run a black box, then decode. Even your Huffman coder fit this mold. The black box keeps changing, but the wrapper never does.
