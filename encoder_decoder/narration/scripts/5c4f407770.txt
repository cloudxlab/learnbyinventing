Imagine you've just been handed a real task at work. There's a recommendation engine your company bought, and it only understands integer user IDs, but your data identifies people by email address. You can't change the engine, so the plan is to wrap it: an encoder turns emails into numbers, and a decoder turns the answers back into emails. Think about what has to be saved between those two steps. Let's dig in.
