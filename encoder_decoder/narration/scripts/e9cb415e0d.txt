# Persist the Mapping The Decoder is a separate program. Write save_mapping and load_mapping... Hint 1... Hint 2... Solution...

Here's a key insight: the encoder and decoder are separate programs. They don't share memory. So the mapping between emails and integers has to be saved to a file. Write two functions: one to save the mapping and one to load it back. The real test is the round trip. If you save and load, you should get back exactly what you started with, types and all.
