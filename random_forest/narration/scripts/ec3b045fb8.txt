Here's a challenge before we go any further. Can you invent a single number that tells us how spread out a group of values is? It should be zero when every value is identical, and it should grow as the values drift apart.

Pause here and try to come up with your own formula. Think about what you'd do with the distances from the average, and what could go wrong if some are above and some are below.

When you're ready, we'll compare notes. Then you can write your version in code.
