Now assemble everything into a DecisionTree class with init, fit, and predict methods. Fit grows the tree recursively starting at depth zero. A group becomes a leaf, whose value is the mean of its targets, when any stopping condition fires: depth has reached max_depth, the group has fewer than min_samples_split rows, or find_best_split finds no split with positive reduction. Otherwise, split the rows on the best feature and threshold and recurse into each side with depth plus one. Predict walks each row from the root, going left if the feature value is at most the threshold, and returns the leaf's value.
