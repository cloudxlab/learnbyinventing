Randomness makes each tree different, so their errors are independent --- averaging independent errors cancels them out, while averaging identical errors would not C. Here's a quick check on the big idea behind random forests. Take a moment and think about it before you pick an answer. We're adding randomness in two places: which rows each tree sees, and which features it can consider. Why would that make the final prediction better? Think about what happens when a bunch of trees all make the exact same mistake, compared to when they each make different mistakes.
