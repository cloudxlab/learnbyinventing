Take this group of values: ten, nine, eleven, eight, ten, and one. It has one troublemaker. Split it into a left group with the first five values and a right group with just the one. Now left is nearly pure and right is exactly pure since a single value has zero variance. But there is a trap: a one-element group having zero variance should not count as much as a five-element group having zero variance, otherwise the tree just slices off one element at a time. Your challenge is to combine the two variances so that a larger group counts more. Write a function called total_variance that takes left and right.
