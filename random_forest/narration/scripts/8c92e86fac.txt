Your deep tree scored a perfect zero error on its training data. That should make you suspicious, not proud, because it just memorized. So here's the big idea: train lots of trees and average their predictions. Their mistakes point in different directions and cancel out, while the real signal survives. But there's a catch. If every tree sees the same data, they'll all be identical, and averaging identical trees gives you one tree. Can you think of where we could add randomness?
