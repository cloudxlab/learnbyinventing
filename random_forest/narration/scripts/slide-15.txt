Here's a problem with our forest so far. Even with different bootstrap samples, the data still looks pretty similar, so most trees will grab the same strongest feature, size, for their very first question. That makes the crowd too agreeable. So here's our second dose of randomness: each tree only gets to see a random handful of the features. Try writing a function that picks distinct feature indices at random. Python's random module is your friend here. Give it a shot before moving on.
