Here's a group with one troublemaker, and we've sliced it into two pieces. One piece is nearly pure, and the other is perfectly pure, since a single value has zero variance. But look closer, because there's a trap. A lone element having zero variance shouldn't count as much as a big group being perfectly tidy. Otherwise, the tree just slices off one element at a time. So how would you combine the two variances? Think about what fraction of the elements each side holds, then try writing it yourself.
