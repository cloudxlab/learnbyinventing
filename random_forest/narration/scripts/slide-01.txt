Last time, our tree asked a simple question: how mixed up are the labels in this group? Now we're predicting numbers, like house prices, so the question changes a little. How spread out are the values? Look at these two groups. If a leaf holds houses that all cost about the same, guessing the average works great. But if the prices are all over the place, that average is wrong for everyone. Keep that idea in mind.
