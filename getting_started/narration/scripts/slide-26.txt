That's a wrap on the basics, and this slide is your cheat sheet. It's got how to run a cell, add a new one, print, store values, do arithmetic, write text, and read an error message. You don't need to memorize any of it. Come back whenever you forget something. And remember, when something breaks, start with the last line of the error. It usually tells you exactly what went wrong.
