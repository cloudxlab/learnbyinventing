# ON SCREEN:
# Reflection. Open-ended questions: What was your reaction to your first error? Interpreter vs notebook - when is each useful? What does 2 ** 0.5 give? How would you explain a variable to a friend?
# ---
Before moving on, take a moment with these reflection questions. There are no right or wrong answers here. Think about how you felt when you first saw an error message. Consider when you would use the interpreter versus a notebook. Try typing 2 to the power of 0.5 and see what you get. And think about how you would explain what a variable is to a friend. These questions help you lock in what you just learned.
