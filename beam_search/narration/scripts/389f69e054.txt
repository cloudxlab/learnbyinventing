# A large language model (LLM) does something remarkably simple: Given some text, it predicts probabilities for the next token .
# [Fragment 1]: The simplest strategy: always pick the most probable token . This is called greedy decoding .

The simplest idea is to just take the top choice every single time. Whichever token has the highest probability wins, and that's called greedy decoding. It's quick and easy, but let's see whether always grabbing the best-looking option actually leads to the best sentence.
