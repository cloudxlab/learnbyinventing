# A large language model (LLM) does something remarkably simple: Given some text, it predicts probabilities for the next token .
# [Fragment 1]: The simplest strategy: always pick the most probable token . This is called greedy decoding .
# ---
The simplest idea is to just take whichever token the model thinks is most likely, every single time. That's called greedy decoding. It's fast and easy, but hold onto a question: is always grabbing the best-looking option right now actually the best way to build a whole sentence?
