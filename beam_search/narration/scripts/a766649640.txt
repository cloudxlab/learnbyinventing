# The idea: instead of keeping one candidate at each step, keep the top [math] best candidates.
# ---
Here's a thought: what if we didn't have to bet everything on a single choice at each step? Instead of keeping just one candidate, we hold on to the few best ones. We'll call that number k, and it's our beam width. That way, a path that looks a little weaker right now still gets a chance to prove itself later.
