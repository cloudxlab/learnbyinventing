# The idea: instead of keeping one candidate at each step, keep the top [math] best candidates.
# [Fragment 1]: It s like exploring a tree, but instead of one path (greedy) or all paths (exhaustive), you keep [math] beams of light shining through the most promising branches. This is called beam search , and [math] is the beam width .
# ---
Think of it as shining a handful of flashlights down the most promising branches, instead of just one path or every path. That's beam search, and the number of flashlights you keep is called the beam width.
