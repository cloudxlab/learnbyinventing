# Part 5: Sampling with Weighted Choices
# ---
You've now seen how beam search keeps several good candidates alive instead of betting on just one. But there's a catch we haven't touched yet: always picking the highest-scoring option can make the output predictable and repetitive. So in this part, we'll explore a different idea. What if we choose randomly, but let the probabilities guide how likely each choice is? Let's see how that works.
