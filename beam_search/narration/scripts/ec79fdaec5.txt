Until now, our model has always picked the single best token, which is predictable but can get repetitive and dull. So here's a new idea: what if we roll weighted dice instead? We sample from the probability distribution. The most likely token still wins most of the time, but less likely tokens get a real chance too. Think about what that might do to the text.
