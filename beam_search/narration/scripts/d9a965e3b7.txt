# ON SCREEN:
# Part 7: The Problem with Greedy Choices
# ---
Up to now, beam search has been our fix for greedy choices. But let's slow down and ask why greedy goes wrong in the first place. Picking the single best option at every step feels sensible, doesn't it? Yet sometimes the best first step leads somewhere worse. Before we move on, think about a time when grabbing the obvious winner right now cost you later.
