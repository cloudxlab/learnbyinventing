# ON SCREEN:
# Part 3: Weighted Random Selection
# ---
We've seen how beam search keeps several promising candidates alive instead of just one. Now there's a new question: should we always grab the very best option, or could there be value in sometimes picking a lower-ranked one? In this part, we'll explore weighted random selection. It's a way to choose among options based on how likely each one is, rather than always taking the top. Let's see how it works.
