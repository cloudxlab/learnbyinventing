Let's try something different: picking randomly, but with a twist. Imagine a spinner split into sections of different sizes. Section A takes up most of the wheel, B gets a smaller slice, and C gets just a sliver.

Before we spin it, look at where each section starts and stops along the line from zero to one. How could a single random number between zero and one tell us which section we landed in?
