# ON SCREEN:
# Imagine a spinner divided into sections of different sizes: Item Probability Range on spinner A 0.7 0.0 0.7 B 0.2 0.7 0.9 C 0.1 0.9 1.0
# [Fragment 1]: The technique: walk through the probabilities, accumulating a running total. Stop when the random number is less than the running total.
# ---
Here's the trick: we walk through the items one at a time, adding up their probabilities as we go. The moment our random number falls below that running total, we stop, and that's the item we pick.
