# ON SCREEN:
# We want a knob that controls randomness: Low randomness : almost always pick the best token (like greedy) High randomness : pick more adventurously from unlikely tokens
# ---
Here's a puzzle. Sometimes we want the model to play it safe and pick the most likely word. Other times, we want it to take a few risks and surprise us. Wouldn't it be great to have one dial that controls how adventurous it gets?

Turn it low, and the model almost always picks the best token, just like greedy. Turn it high, and it's willing to gamble on the unlikely ones. Before we move on, think about how you'd build that dial.
