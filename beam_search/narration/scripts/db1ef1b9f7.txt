# ON SCREEN:
# We want a knob that controls randomness: Low randomness : almost always pick the best token (like greedy) High randomness : pick more adventurously from unlikely tokens
# ---
We've seen that greedy always grabs the top token, and sampling can wander too far. So what we really want is a dial, a knob we can turn to decide how adventurous the model should be. Turn it one way, and the model almost always picks its best guess, just like greedy. Turn it the other way, and it starts taking chances on unlikely tokens. Let's think about how we'd build that.
