# ON SCREEN:
# Going further: Pseudo-Huber loss, Quantile loss, Log-cosh loss, varying delta
# ---
If you want to explore further, there are several interesting directions. The Pseudo-Huber loss is a smooth approximation that avoids even the transition point. Quantile loss lets you weight over-predictions and under-predictions differently. And log-cosh is another smooth alternative. Also, try varying delta in your Huber loss and see how the curve changes. These are all tools you might encounter in practice.
