So here's where we've landed. Squared loss is smooth and easy to work with for small errors, but its gradient explodes when the error is large. Absolute loss keeps the gradient bounded, but it has that sharp corner at zero.

Each one is good exactly where the other struggles. So take a moment and think: what if we used squared loss near zero and absolute loss far away? How might you stitch the two together?
