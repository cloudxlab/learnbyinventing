Welcome! Imagine you've built a model that predicts house prices. Some of its guesses come out too high, others too low. How do you tell, in one single number, how wrong it is overall? That's the number we need, because it lets us nudge each weight and watch the error change. By the end of this chapter, you'll have invented that number yourself, from scratch.
