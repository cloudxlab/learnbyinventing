# Answer: C. The V-shape of |x| has a sharp corner at x = 0 where the slope from the left (-1) and slope from the right (+1) disagree. No single tangent line exists there.
# ---
The answer is C, at zero. The absolute value function makes that sharp V-shape right at the origin. If you approach from the left, the slope is negative one. From the right, it's positive one. They disagree, so there's no single tangent line you can draw at that point. The derivative simply doesn't exist there, and that's a real problem for gradient descent.
