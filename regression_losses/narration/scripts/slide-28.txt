You've now built a loss function from scratch, and there's plenty more to explore. Other people have invented smooth cousins of Huber, like pseudo-Huber and log-cosh, which avoid even that small bend at the transition point. There's also quantile loss, where you penalize under-predicting and over-predicting differently, for when one kind of mistake costs more.
