It's B, a hundred times larger. When you square an error of ten, you get a hundred. Square an error of one, you just get one. So the big error contributes a hundred times more to the total loss. That's the key insight about MSE: squaring amplifies large mistakes way more than small ones. One bad prediction can completely dominate your loss.
