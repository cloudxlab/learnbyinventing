Here's a simple idea for building our own loss function. Pick a cutoff, and call it d. When the error is small, use the squared formula. When it's big, switch to the absolute one. Easy, right?

Before we trust it, let's test it right at the switch point, where the error equals one. Work out the value from each formula there. Then work out the gradient from each. Are they equal?

Try it on paper first. Then see if you can spot the problem.
