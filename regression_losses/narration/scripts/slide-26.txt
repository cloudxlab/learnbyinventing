# What You Invented

Look at what you just built. You started with a simple question: how wrong is a prediction? Step by step, you invented a way to turn mistakes into a single number. You noticed that positive and negative errors cancel out, so you fixed that, and you learned why big misses should hurt more than small ones. Those are the same ideas experts use every day, and you found them yourself. Let's see what they're called.
