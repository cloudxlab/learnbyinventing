# ---
Here's a challenge that ties two ideas together. You've got a loss that's just the error squared, and your job is to find its gradient using the power rule. Do that by hand first.

Then check yourself like a scientist. Pick a few values, like negative two, zero, one, and three. Nudge each one by a tiny amount, and see how much the loss changes. That's the finite difference. If your formula is right, the two answers should match. Give it a try before moving on.
