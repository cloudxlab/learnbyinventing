The answer is B, negative one. The three differences are plus one, minus two, and zero. Add them up and you get negative one. But the model is clearly off on two of the three houses! The problem is that the positive and negative errors cancelled each other out, hiding the true amount of error. That's exactly why raw sums don't work as an error measure.
