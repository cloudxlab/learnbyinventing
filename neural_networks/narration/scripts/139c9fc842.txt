# The step function The bluntest squasher possible: returns if , else . Write it. (In 1958 this exact function, wrapped around a weighted sum, was called the perceptron — the first artificial neuron.) Your task: Implement .
# ---
Time to build your first artificial neuron, and it's a surprisingly simple one. The step function is the bluntest squasher there is: it looks at a number and says "yes" or "no," with nothing in between. Before you code it, think about where the cutoff sits and what each side returns. Give it a try yourself. Fun fact: back in 1958, this exact function wrapped around a weighted sum was called the perceptron.
