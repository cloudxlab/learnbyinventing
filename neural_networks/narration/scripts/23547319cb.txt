Here's a thought: the line failed because it tried to cover everyone, and half those people pay nothing. So what if we stop asking one model to do both jobs? The classifier already knows who's taxable. Now fit a straight line using only those people. It should be perfect, and the check is that an income of ten thousand lands on fifteen hundred in tax. Then chain the two together, compare the error with the plain line, and see what happens. Give it a go yourself first. And notice what you've built: one model deciding which expert answers.
