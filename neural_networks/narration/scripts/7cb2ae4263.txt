Now write the training loop. You've done this before — compute the gradient for each weight using finite differences, then step downhill. But here's the twist: when you run it on the raw heights, something strange happens. The error starts at 0.6 and stays at 0.6. One hundred epochs, and zero progress.

Before moving on, try to figure out why. Print the sigmoid of m times 171 plus c. That's sigmoid of about 20, which is 0.99999999. Every prediction is stuck on the flat top of the sigmoid curve where the slope is essentially zero. No slope means no gradient means no learning. This is called saturation, and it's the single most classic failure mode in neural networks.
