Here's something worth pausing on: what you just built has a real name. It's called logistic regression, and it's exactly one neuron. Take a weighted sum, add a bias, then squash the result through an activation function.

And remember that centering trick you used? That's why every deep learning tutorial starts by normalizing inputs.
