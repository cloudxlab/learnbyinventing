Step two: for just the taxable people, the relationship between income and tax is a perfect straight line. Fit that line on only those rows, then chain the two models together into one prediction function. If the classifier says taxable, return the line's prediction. Otherwise, return zero. Compare its error to the plain line's from before.

What you just invented is called a gated model — one network decides which expert handles each input. Scaled up, this idea is called a mixture of experts, and it's actually used inside several of today's frontier large language models.
