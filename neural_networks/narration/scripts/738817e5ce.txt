# Prove the line fails Fit the best straight line to (x, y). Compute its RMSE and look at what it predicts for low incomes. The line predicts negative tax for low incomes. The shape of the model is wrong.
# ---
Let's prove that a straight line simply cannot solve this problem. Use polyfit to find the best line, then compute its RMSE and look at what it predicts for people with low incomes. You'll see two damning results: the RMSE stays above 0.1 no matter what, and for incomes below 3000, the line predicts negative tax — as if the government is paying you. The fitting isn't the problem. The shape of the model is fundamentally wrong for this data.
