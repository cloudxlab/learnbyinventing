The answer is B. For positive inputs, ReLU's derivative is always exactly one — the gradient never vanishes no matter how large the input gets. Compare that to sigmoid, whose derivative shrinks toward zero for large inputs. That's the saturation problem you experienced firsthand. ReLU simply doesn't have that issue on the positive side.
