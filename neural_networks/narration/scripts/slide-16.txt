Here's where it all comes together. We've got a classifier that can tell who owes tax and who doesn't. Now, for just the taxable people, the relationship is a perfect straight line, so fit one on only those rows. Then chain the two models: if the classifier says taxable, use the line, otherwise return zero. Try building it yourself and compare its error with the plain line from before. You've just invented a gated model, one network deciding which expert handles each input. Scaled up, that's mixture of experts, which powers some of today's biggest language models.
