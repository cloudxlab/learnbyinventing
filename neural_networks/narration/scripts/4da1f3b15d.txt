The answer is B. When the input to sigmoid is large and positive or large and negative, the output is nearly flat — almost exactly one or almost exactly zero. That means the derivative is practically zero, so gradient descent has no signal to follow. This is the saturation problem, and it's the reason we centered our inputs.
