# What You Just Invented — mapping table: step function to perceptron, sigmoid to logistic regression, saturation, computation graph, mixture of experts, fit_net to training a neural network, relu to default activation.
# ---
Let's take a step back and look at what you built. You started with a step function wrapped around a weighted sum — that's the perceptron from 1958. You smoothed it into sigmoid and trained it with gradient descent — that's logistic regression, which is exactly one neuron. You hit the saturation wall and fixed it by centering your inputs.

Then you chained small functions together into a computation graph, built a classify-then-regress gate — which at scale is called a mixture of experts — and generalized your training loop to work on any list of parameters. Finally, you discovered ReLU, the default activation of modern deep learning. That's the real thing. Every neural network framework works on exactly these principles.
