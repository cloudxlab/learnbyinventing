# ON SCREEN:
# What is the square of 3? Of 4? Of 5?
# [Fragment 2]: sqrt(16) = 4 , sqrt(25) = 5 , sqrt(9) = 3
# ---
Look at that: the square root just runs the squaring backwards. Nine goes back to three, sixteen to four, twenty-five to five. So a square root is simply the question, "what number times itself gives me this?"
