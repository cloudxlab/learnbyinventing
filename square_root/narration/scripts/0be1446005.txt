# What is the square root of 5? It s not a whole number. But you already know something about it.
# ---
You already know how to find square roots of perfect squares, like four or nine. But what about five? It doesn't land on a whole number. Still, you know something about it. Take a moment and think: where would it have to sit? Jot down your guess before we move on.
