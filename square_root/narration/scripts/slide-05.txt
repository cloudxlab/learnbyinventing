# Part 2: The Guessing Game You know sqrt(5) is between 2 and 3. Let's find it by guessing, checking, and narrowing down.

Welcome to Part 2! So far we know the square root of five has to be somewhere between two and three. Now let's play a game. We'll make a guess, check how close it is, and use that to narrow things down. It's a bit like "guess my number," and it's how you'll invent your own square root finder.
