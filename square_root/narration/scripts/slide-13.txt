Here's something that might surprise you: every single guess you make cuts the remaining range in half. After just five guesses, you're down to one thirty-second of where you started. After ten, one over a thousand. And by twenty guesses, the range has shrunk to about one in a million. That's the real power of halving: it shrinks the problem incredibly fast.
