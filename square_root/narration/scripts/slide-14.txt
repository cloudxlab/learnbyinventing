# ON SCREEN:
# Every guess eliminates half of the remaining range. After 1 guess: half the range left After 5 guesses: 1/32 of the range After 10 guesses: 1/1024 of the range After 20 guesses: 1/1,048,576 of the range
# ---
Here's something that should feel a little magical. Every single guess throws away half of the remaining range. After just five guesses, you're down to one thirty-second of where you started. After ten, one over a thousand. And after twenty guesses? You've shrunk the search to about one in a million. Let that sink in.
