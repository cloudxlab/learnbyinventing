# Here are the names computer scientists settled on: What you experienced What it's called The game (CartPole) Environment The 4 numbers you see each step Observations (or state ) Push left / push right Actions Number of steps survived Reward Your function () A policy Play game → see result → tweak policy → repeat Reinforcement Learning This is the closest thing to how humans learn. You don't get a textbook — you try stuff, get feedback, and adapt. A child learning to walk, a dog learning tricks, you learning to drive — all reinforcement learning. That's life!
# ---
Here's the vocabulary people use for everything you just did. The game is the environment. The four numbers you saw each step are observations, and pushing left or right are your actions. How long you survived is the reward, and your function is called a policy. Play, see, tweak, repeat: that's reinforcement learning. It's how children learn to walk and how you learned to drive. You've been doing it all along.
