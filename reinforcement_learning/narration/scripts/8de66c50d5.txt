Think about the difference between searching for good weights from the outside, like with a genetic algorithm, versus learning from experience inside the game, like REINFORCE and Actor-Critic do. Consider why the credit assignment problem is so hard, and how discounted rewards and TD errors each try to solve it. Think about the actor-critic architecture, with its doer and evaluator working together.
