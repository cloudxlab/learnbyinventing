# Strategy 1 — Random Search Generate 100 random weight vectors (each weight between -1 and 1). Evaluate each one over 5 episodes. Print the best weights and their score.
# ---
Time to try your first real learning strategy, and it's a simple one: pure guesswork. Generate a hundred random weight vectors, each number between negative one and one. Then test every one over five episodes, and keep whichever scored best. It's a bit like throwing darts blindfolded, but with enough throws, you might get lucky. Give it a go yourself, then see how high your best score climbs.
