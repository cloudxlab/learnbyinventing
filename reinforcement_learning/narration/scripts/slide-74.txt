You've just built a whole reinforcement learning toolkit, and now you get to see the real names for it. Your hand-coded rules? Heuristic policies. Random weights, then mutation? Random search and genetic algorithms. Tweaking weights and measuring the score? That's policy gradients, and REINFORCE. Your critic is the value function. So you've invented actor-critic yourself.
