# Why Probabilities? Your current policy is deterministic: if , always go right. Always. Every single time. If the policy always makes the same decision in the same state, can it ever discover that the OTHER action might have been better? # ---
Here's a thought experiment. Right now, your policy does the exact same thing in the same situation, every single time. So how would it ever find out that the other action might be better? It wouldn't. It's stuck.

Now imagine the model says "go right seventy percent of the time." Occasionally it goes left, purely by accident. Think about what you could learn from those accidents. If one of them pays off better than usual, what should the model do next time? Take a moment and think it through.
