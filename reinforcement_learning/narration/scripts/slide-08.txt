# Develop Your Intuition Before writing any strategy, just think: The pole is tilting to the RIGHT. Should you push the cart left or right to keep it balanced? The pole is tilting to the LEFT. Which way should you push? The pole is nearly vertical but moving (rotating) fast to the right. What should you do?
# ---
Before we write any code, let's just think like a person for a moment. Imagine you're holding that pole upright on a cart. If it's tipping to the right, which way would you shove the cart? Now flip it: what if it's tipping left? And here's the tricky one: the pole's nearly vertical, but spinning fast to the right. What then? Pause and try answering each one yourself. Your gut instincts are the start of your strategy.
