# Assigning Credit You played a game and scored 150 steps. At step 100, you pushed right. Was pushing right at step 100 a good action? How would you know? Which reward is more relevant to the action at step 100 — the reward at step 101 or the reward at step 150? If you got a reward of 1 at each step, how would you assign 'credit' to the action at step 100? Should nearby rewards count more than distant ones?
# ---
Here's a game you just played: 150 steps, and somewhere in the middle, at step 100, you pushed right. Was that a good move? Honestly, you can't tell from that single moment. The consequences unfold over time. So think about it: which reward says more about that push, the very next one or the one way out at step 150? And if every step pays one point, how would you share the credit? Take a moment and try it.
