Remember those rules of thumb you came up with in Part 2? Let's look at them differently. Whatever your best one was, it was really doing something simple: taking each observation, multiplying it by some number, adding everything up, and checking whether the total is positive or negative. That's it. Your action was just a function of what you observed.
