Time to put it all together. You're going to write a function that plays one complete game using your probabilistic policy, and keeps a careful record as it goes.

At every step, you'll compute the probability, sample an action from it, work out the gradient for that action, and save the observation, action, reward, and gradient.

Take a moment to try it yourself. When it works, play one episode and print the steps and total reward.
