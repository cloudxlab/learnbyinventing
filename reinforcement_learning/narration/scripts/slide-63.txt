You just invented several things at once, and they all have real names. A model that predicts future reward from a state is called the value function, or the critic. Training it step by step is Temporal Difference learning, and the error you computed is the advantage, or TD error. Notice the critic never picks actions. It only judges how well the policy is doing. DQN is a close cousin that predicts the value of each action instead, but the core TD idea is exactly what you built.
