# Develop Your Intuition Before writing any strategy, just think: The pole is tilting to the RIGHT. Should you push the cart left or right to keep it balanced? The pole is tilting to the LEFT. Which way should you push? The pole is nearly vertical but moving (rotating) fast to the right. What should you do?
# ---
Before we write any code, let's just think like a balancer. Picture holding a broom upright on your palm. If it starts tipping to the right, which way do you move your hand? What if it's tipping left? Now the tricky one: the pole looks nearly vertical, but it's spinning quickly to the right. What would you do then? Take a minute to think it through, because your gut instincts here are the seeds of your strategy.
