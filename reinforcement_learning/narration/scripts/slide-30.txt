# Part 6: Can Gradients Help? Genetic algorithms work but feel brute-force. You might remember a more targeted approach: compute the gradient, move in the direction that improves the objective. Can we do that here?

We've seen that genetic algorithms can find good solutions, but let's be honest, they feel a bit like guessing and checking at scale. So here's a question worth pausing on. Remember gradients? Instead of trying random variations, you compute which direction improves things and step that way. Do you think we could do that here? Take a moment and think about it.
