# ON SCREEN:
# The CartPole environment: keep the pole balanced by pushing the cart left or right.
# ---
Here's a challenge to start us off: a pole balanced on a cart, like trying to balance a broomstick on your palm. It's called CartPole. The pole wants to fall over, and your only controls are pushing the cart left or right. Before we learn anything fancy, think about it for a moment. If the pole starts tipping right, which way should you push, and why?
