You just invented REINFORCE, one of the foundational algorithms in reinforcement learning. Think about what happened. The model started knowing nothing, basically flipping coins. It played, made random choices, and good accidents got amplified while bad ones got reversed. After hundreds of games, it found a good strategy. Nobody ever taught it the rules of physics. It learned entirely from its own experience, just like you did earlier, only automatically.
