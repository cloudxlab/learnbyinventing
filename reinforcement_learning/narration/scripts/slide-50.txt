Now it's time to put all your pieces together into one learning rule. Think of it as a recipe for learning from a single game. Play an episode, compute your discounted rewards, normalize them, and weight each step's gradient by its normalized reward. Then sum everything up and take a step. Notice we're climbing uphill here, so it's ascent, not descent. Try writing it yourself first.
