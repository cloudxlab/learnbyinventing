Here's a question to think through before we go on. Your model just played a game, and now you know which actions earned good rewards and which earned bad ones. How should it learn from that?

Take "go right." If it paid off, should the model make that action more likely or less likely next time? And if it turned out badly? Try answering both on your own.

You'll also need to decide whether to move with the gradient or against it. And there's one more puzzle. Probabilities multiply across many steps, which gets awkward. Is there a function that turns products into sums? Give that a guess before we continue.
