That's what you just built, and it has a name: a Genetic Algorithm. Look at how neatly the pieces line up with biology. Your weight vector is like DNA, the score is fitness, keeping the top twenty is natural selection, and adding noise to make children is mutation. Repeat that for generations, and you've got evolution. You found good policy parameters without any calculus, just random variation and selection. Pretty remarkable, right?
