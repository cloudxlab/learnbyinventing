Here's the good news: the whole world of our pole-balancing game fits into just four numbers. Where the cart is on the track, how fast it's moving, which way the pole is leaning, and how fast that pole is tipping over.

And you only get two moves: push left, or push right. Think about it: with just that much information, could you decide what to do?
