# ON SCREEN:
# The CartPole environment: keep the pole balanced by pushing the cart left or right.
# ---
Here's a game you've probably seen before: a pole is balanced on top of a little cart, and your only job is to keep it from falling over. You can't touch the pole. All you can do is push the cart left or right. Take a moment to think about it. If the pole starts tipping to the right, which way would you push?
