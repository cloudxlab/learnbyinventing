# RL Is Everywhere Map the RL terms to a real-life example: a child learning to ride a bicycle. What is the environment? What are the observations (state)? What are the actions? What are the rewards and penalties? What is the policy?
# ---
Here's a fun one: you've probably already done reinforcement learning yourself. Think about a child learning to ride a bicycle. Try mapping each RL term to that story. What's the environment? What does the child observe? What actions can they take? What counts as a reward, and what counts as a penalty? And what's the policy? Pause here and try it before we move on.
