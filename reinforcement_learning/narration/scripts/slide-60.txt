# ---
Here's a puzzle. Your value model needs the discounted return, but that means waiting for the whole game to finish. That's slow, and sometimes the game never really ends. So can we cheat a little?

Think about what you do have: the reward right now, and the value model's guess for the next state. The return at this step is the reward plus a discounted return from the next step. And the model already estimates that next part. Try filling in that blank yourself.
