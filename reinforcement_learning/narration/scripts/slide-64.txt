You've built two separate pieces. There's the actor, which picks actions, and the critic, which judges how good a state is. Now here's a question: what if they worked together? The critic could tell the actor how well its actions are really going, and the actor could use that feedback to improve. As the actor plays better, the critic gets better at judging, too. They grow together.
