Here's something to think about. Right now, your policy is completely deterministic. In the same situation, it makes the same choice, every single time. So how could it ever find out that the other action might actually be better?

What if the model only went right seventy percent of the time? Now and then it would go left, purely by accident. Pause for a moment and think about what you could learn from those accidents. If one of them paid off better than usual, what should the model do next time?
