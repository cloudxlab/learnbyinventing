# Strategy 1 — Random Search Generate 100 random weight vectors (each weight between -1 and 1). Evaluate each one over 5 episodes. Print the best weights and their score.
# ---
Time to try your first strategy, and it's the simplest one you can think of: just guess. Generate a hundred random sets of weights, each number somewhere between negative one and one. Then test every set over five episodes and keep whichever scored best. It's a bit like throwing darts blindfolded. Give it a go yourself, and see how high a score pure luck can get you.
