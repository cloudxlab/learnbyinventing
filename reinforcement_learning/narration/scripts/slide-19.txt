A quick note before we go further. You'll see a lot of lambdas created inside loops from here on, and there's a small Python gotcha hiding in them. Without a special trick, every lambda in the loop would share the same variable and use whatever value it ended up with at the end. The trick is to capture the current value at the moment each lambda is created. Keep an eye out for it, because you'll meet it again in several exercises.
