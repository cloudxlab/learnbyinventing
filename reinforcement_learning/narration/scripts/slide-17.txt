# RL Is Everywhere Map the RL terms to a real-life example: a child learning to ride a bicycle. What is the environment? What are the observations (state)? What are the actions? What are the rewards and penalties? What is the policy?
# ---
Here's something fun: reinforcement learning isn't just for robots and game-playing computers. It's happening all around us, all the time. Think about a child learning to ride a bicycle. Can you map our RL terms onto that? What's the environment? What does the child observe? What actions can they take? What counts as a reward, or a penalty? And what's the policy? Pause here and try it yourself.
