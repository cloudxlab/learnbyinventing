Now let's make our policy a little smarter. Instead of looking at just the angle, we'll give it two clues: the angle and how fast the pole is swinging. Each one gets its own weight, and you add them up to get a score. If it's below zero, go right; otherwise, go left. Try those four weight combinations, and write down each average score. Notice which ones do best, and see if you can guess why.
