Here's a typical Actor-Critic learning curve, and it's worth comparing it to the REINFORCE curve we saw earlier. Notice that it climbs faster, and it's much smoother, with far less wobbling. Why? The critic gives the actor feedback at every single step, instead of making it wait until the episode ends. Less waiting means less noise, and learning gets steadier.
