Here's the world your agent lives in. Everything it knows comes down to just four numbers: where the cart is, how fast it's moving, which way the pole is leaning, and how fast that pole is falling. That's the whole picture.

And it only has two moves: push left, or push right. Before we go on, think about it. If the pole starts tipping right, which way would you push?
