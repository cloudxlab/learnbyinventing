# Assigning Credit You played a game and scored 150 steps. At step 100, you pushed right. Was pushing right at step 100 a good action? How would you know? Which reward is more relevant to the action at step 100 — the reward at step 101 or the reward at step 150? If you got a reward of 1 at each step, how would you assign 'credit' to the action at step 100? Should nearby rewards count more than distant ones?
# ---
Here's a question that sits at the heart of reinforcement learning. Imagine you played a game and lasted a hundred and fifty steps. Way back at step a hundred, you pushed right. Was that a good move? Think about it: which reward tells you more about that push, the one right after it, or the one at the very end? And should nearby rewards count more than far-away ones? Try sketching your own way to hand out credit before we move on.
