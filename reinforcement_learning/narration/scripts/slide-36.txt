# Design Your Own Credit System You agreed that nearby rewards should count more than distant ones. Now design a formula. Suppose you played 5 steps and got rewards [1, 1, 1, 1, 1]. You want to compute a "total credit" for the action at step 0. If you just sum all future rewards equally, what's the credit for step 0? (1+1+1+1+1 = 5). What's the problem with this? What if each future reward was worth a little LESS than the one before it — say 99% as much? Step 0's own reward counts fully (×1), step 1's reward counts ×0.99, step 2's counts ×0.99², etc. What would the credit for step 0 be? What should the credit for the LAST step (step 4) be? It has no future rewards. What would you call that 0.99 multiplier?
# ---
Here's a tricky one. Say you played five steps and got a reward of one each time. If you add them all up equally, step zero gets a credit of five. Pause and think about why that might be a problem. Now imagine each future reward is worth just a little less than the one before, say ninety-nine percent as much. What would step zero's credit be? And what about the last step, which has no future rewards at all? Work it out on paper first. Then, see if you can name that ninety-nine percent multiplier.
