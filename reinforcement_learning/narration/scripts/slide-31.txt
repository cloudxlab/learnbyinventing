# Strategy 3 — Tweak and Observe Here's the idea: for each weight, nudge it slightly (+0.01), play a few games, nudge it the other way (-0.01), play again. The difference in scores tells you which direction improves that weight. This is called finite differences — estimating the gradient by measuring the effect of small changes. Take your best weights from the genetic algorithm (or start with ). Estimate the gradient and update for 20 steps. Does the score improve?
# ---
Here's a third idea, and it's a clever one. Take one weight and nudge it up a tiny bit. Play a few games. Then nudge it down, and play again. Whichever direction scored better tells you which way to move that weight.

Do that for every weight, and you've estimated the gradient just by measuring. Start from your best genetic algorithm weights, run twenty steps, and see if the score climbs. Give it a try!
