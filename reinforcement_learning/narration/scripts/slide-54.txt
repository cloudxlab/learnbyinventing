Quick heads-up before you start training: expect some noise. Reinforcement learning is bumpy by nature, so your scores will bounce around, and sometimes performance even dips before it recovers. That's normal. If your scores collapse and stay low, just re-run the training cell, because a different random seed can change everything. This instability is exactly why researchers came up with the improvements we'll invent in the next two parts.
