Time to put your idea to work. Write a loop that keeps picking a random action, zero or one, until the game ends. Count every step you survive, and that count is your score.

Before you run it, take a guess: how long do you think random flailing will last? Then run it a few times and compare. Do the scores stay close together, or do they jump around?

Give it a real try before moving on. Whatever numbers you see will be the benchmark we try to beat.
