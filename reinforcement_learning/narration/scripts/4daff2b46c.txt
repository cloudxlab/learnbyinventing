# You Can Already Predict Think about two situations mid-game: The pole is nearly vertical, barely moving. How many more steps do you think you'll survive? What's your expected future reward from here? The pole is nearly horizontal, falling fast. Expected future reward? You just made an intuitive prediction about future reward based on the state. If a MODEL could make this prediction, why would that be useful for learning?
# ---
Here's something you already do without thinking about it. Picture the pole nearly upright and barely moving. You'd guess you can survive a long time. Now picture it nearly flat and dropping fast. That's about to end. Take a moment to put rough numbers on both. Then ask yourself: if a model could make that guess, how would it help us learn?
