Here's a puzzle. With only one bit, you've got just two possible codes, zero and one. But now we have three symbols, so something's gotta give. One idea is the simplest fix: give every symbol two bits. A, B, and C each get their own pair, and one pair goes unused. Try writing out a couple of messages with this scheme yourself before moving on.
