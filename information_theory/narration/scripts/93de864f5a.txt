Huffman gets us really close to entropy, but not exactly there. We're at 1.70 bits per symbol, and the theoretical limit is about 1.69. Why the small gap? Think about it: Huffman has to hand out whole bits. It can't give a symbol 1.3 bits, only one or two. Take a moment and see if that makes sense to you.
