Here's a question to sit with before you pick an answer. Your Huffman code hit the entropy exactly, 1.75 bits per symbol. Does that mean it always will?

Think about why it worked here. Look at those frequencies: half, a quarter, an eighth, an eighth. Ask yourself whether the code lengths came out as whole numbers by luck, or for a reason. Then try imagining a different set of frequencies, and see whether it still works. Take a moment, then choose.
