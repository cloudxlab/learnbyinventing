Imagine you're a telegraph operator, and the only thing your wire can carry is a zero or a one. That's it. Your challenge is to turn any message into those bits, and make sure the person on the other end can turn it back without any mix-ups.

By the end of this chapter, you'll have invented a method that finds the shortest possible encoding for any alphabet. Let's start building it.
