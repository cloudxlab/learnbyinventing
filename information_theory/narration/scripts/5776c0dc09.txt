# ON SCREEN:
# What You Just Invented
# ---
Look at everything you just built, starting from a simple question: how do we send messages using as few bits as possible? Along the way, you discovered prefix-free codes, so no code is confused with another. You found that common symbols deserve short codes. Then you invented Huffman's greedy tree, and finally entropy, the true limit of compression. Take a moment to see how your ideas match the real names.
