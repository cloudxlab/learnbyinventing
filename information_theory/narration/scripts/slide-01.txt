# Part 1: Two Symbols — A and B You need to transmit messages that contain only the letters A and B . The wire only carries 0s and 1s.
# ---
Let's start small. Imagine you're sending messages that only ever use two letters, A and B. The catch is that the wire can only carry zeros and ones. So how would you turn those letters into something the wire understands? Give it some thought before we go further. There's no single trick here, just your own invention.
