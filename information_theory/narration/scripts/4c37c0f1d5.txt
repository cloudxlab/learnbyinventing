Now that we've got our Huffman tree built, it's time to turn it into actual codes. Here's the idea: start at the top and walk down to each symbol. Every time you go left, you write down one bit, and every time you go right, you write down the other. Think about which bit should go where, and what the path to each symbol would spell out.
