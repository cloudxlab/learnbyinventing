# Part 2: Three Symbols — A, B, and C Now messages contain A , B , and C . You still can only send 0s and 1s.

Now let's make it a little harder. Instead of two symbols, our messages can contain three: A, B, and C. But here's the catch: you can still only send zeros and ones. Before you move on, take a moment to think about it. How would you assign a code to each letter? Is the two-symbol trick still enough?
