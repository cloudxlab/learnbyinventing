You just invented something real. This notation has a name: Big O notation. Computer scientists write it as a capital O, with a simple function of n inside the parentheses, the same kind of shorthand you came up with for describing how an algorithm's work grows. So when you said your slow approach grows like n squared, they'd write that as Big O of n squared. Same idea, just official.
