Now it's time to get your hands dirty. Time both functions on lists of 1,000, 5,000, and 10,000 items, and write down how long each one takes. Then ask yourself: when the input gets five times bigger, how much slower does each function get? Does one barely notice while the other falls behind fast? Try it yourself before moving on. The pattern you spot here is the seed of Big O.
