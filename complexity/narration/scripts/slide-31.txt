Every algorithm carries two price tags: how much time it takes, and how much memory it uses. And now you know how to read both. That's a real skill. As you move through the chapters ahead, you'll run into this tradeoff again and again. Some algorithms are blazing fast because they spend extra memory. Others are slower, but work right in place. Neither is always better, so you'll get to choose.
