Here's the payoff for everything we've been exploring. Picture a million items. The linear function finishes in about a second. The quadratic one? Around eleven days. Same task, wildly different outcomes, just from the shape of the algorithm. Knowing which category yours falls into is one of the most valuable skills you can build as a programmer.
