Here's the difference in real numbers. On a million items, the linear function finishes in about a second. The quadratic one takes around eleven days. Same task, wildly different outcomes, and the only thing that changed is how the work grows as the input gets bigger.

Knowing which category your algorithm falls into is one of the most valuable skills you can build as a programmer. Let's keep going and see why.
