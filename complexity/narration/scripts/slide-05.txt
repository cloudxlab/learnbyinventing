# One function's time grows proportionally to the input size. The other grows proportionally to the square of the input size. This distinction is enormous. On a million items: The linear function takes ~1 second The quadratic function takes ~ 11 days Understanding which category your algorithm falls into is one of the most important skills in programming.
# ---
Here's the thing: these two functions might look almost the same on a small input, but they're nowhere near the same as the input grows. One scales in step with the input size. The other scales with its square. So what happens at a million items? One finishes in about a second. The other takes around eleven days. That gap is huge. Knowing which kind of growth your algorithm has is one of the most valuable skills a programmer can build, and it's exactly what we're about to learn to name.
