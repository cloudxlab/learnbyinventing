Here's a race where every algorithm gets the same starting line. Assume each operation takes just one microsecond. Your job is to fill in the table, working out how many operations each complexity class needs at four input sizes, then turning those counts into real time. Take a few minutes and try it yourself. When you finish, ask which ones still work at a billion items, and which ones are hopeless. The answer may surprise you.
