# ON SCREEN:
# What You Invented
# ---
Look at what you've done. You started by just counting how long things take, with no formulas and no jargon. Then you noticed that what really matters is how the work grows as the input gets bigger, and you learned to ignore the small stuff, like constants and lower-order terms. That's Big O notation, and you invented it yourself. Let's see how your ideas line up with the names experts use.
