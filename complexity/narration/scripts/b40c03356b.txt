# ON SCREEN:
# Reflection questions: counting operations vs timing, what O(n²) means, simplifying expressions, real-world examples, time-space tradeoff, explaining Big O to a friend
# ---
Take your time with these reflection questions. They ask you to explain the core ideas in your own words, which is the best way to check that you truly understand them. Think about why we count operations instead of timing, what O of n squared really means in practice, and how you'd explain Big O to a friend who has never written code. There are no wrong answers here — the point is to articulate what you've discovered.
