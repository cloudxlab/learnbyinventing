# Part 2: Counting Steps Timing is noisy — it depends on your computer's speed, what else is running, even the room temperature. Can we predict how an algorithm grows without running it? Yes: count the key operations .

Part 2 is where we get clever. Timing a program is noisy, because it depends on how fast your computer is, what else is running, and even small things you'd never think to control. So here's a question: can we predict how an algorithm grows without running it at all? We can. The trick is to count the key operations.
