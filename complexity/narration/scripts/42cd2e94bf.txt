Here's what happens when we put real time on those complexity classes. Pretend every operation takes one microsecond. With ten items, everything feels instant. At a thousand, the slowest one starts to show. But look at a billion items. Logarithmic takes thirty microseconds, linear takes about sixteen minutes, and the slowest class takes over thirty years. Take a moment to compare the columns.
