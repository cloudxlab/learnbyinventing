# ON SCREEN:
# Quick Check: Netflix has ~300 million users and ~20,000 titles. Which approach produces a smaller similarity matrix? A. User-user (300M 300M) B. Item-item (20K 20K) C. Both are the same size D. Neither is feasible
# ---
Here's a quick check to test your thinking. Netflix has around three hundred million users, but only about twenty thousand titles. So if you wanted a similarity matrix, you could compare every user to every other user, or every title to every other title.

Before you pick an answer, try working out the rough size of each matrix in your head. Which one is smaller, and by how much? Take a moment, then choose.
