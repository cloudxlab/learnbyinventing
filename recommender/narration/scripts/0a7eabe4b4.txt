# Quick Check: Why do we convert to unit vectors before the matrix multiply? A. To make the matrix invertible B. So that the dot product equals cosine similarity C. To reduce the number of columns D. To remove negative values
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: B. Cosine similarity is [math]. If [math], it simplifies to just [math].
# ---
Right, it's B. Cosine similarity normally means dividing the dot product by the lengths of both vectors. If we shrink every vector to length one ahead of time, that division disappears, and the plain dot product gives us cosine similarity. That's why one matrix multiply can score every pair of people at once.
