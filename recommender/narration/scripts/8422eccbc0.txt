# Now the core: score each candidate movie. The score is a weighted sum people who are more similar to you contribute more to the score of each movie.
# [Fragment 0]: [math] In matrix form, that s just a matrix-vector multiply!

That's the beauty of it: all those weighted sums collapse into one matrix-vector multiply. The similarity scores are the weights, the ratings matrix is what they're applied to, and one multiplication scores every movie at once.
