You just built a recommender, and it has a name: collaborative filtering. It recommends purely from the pattern of who likes what, without knowing anything about the movies themselves. No genres, no actors, no plots. Modern systems blend this with content-based filtering, which compares movie features. And that matrix-product trick for all-pairs similarity? It shows up again inside transformers, where every word compares itself to every other word. Same math, different vectors.
