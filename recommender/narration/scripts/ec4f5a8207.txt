The answer is B. With no ratings, the user's vector is all zeros or all NaN. A zero vector has no direction, so you'd be dividing by zero when computing cosine similarity. This is the famous cold-start problem, and it's why streaming services always ask new users to rate a few titles before generating recommendations.
