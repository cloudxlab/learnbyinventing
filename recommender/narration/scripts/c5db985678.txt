# Sparse MapReduce Matrix Multiplication Your task: write where each argument is a list of triples of the non-zero entries. Return a dict mapping for the non-zero entries of the product.

Here's a real challenge. Your similarity matrix was mostly zeros, and real rating data is usually sparse like that. So how would you multiply two huge matrices without storing or visiting all those zeros? Think about what a mapper could emit for each non-zero entry, and how a reducer could combine them. Give it a try yourself first.
