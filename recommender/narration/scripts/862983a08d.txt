# ON SCREEN:
# Quick Check: Netflix has ~300 million users and ~20,000 titles. Which approach produces a smaller similarity matrix? A. User-user (300M 300M) B. Item-item (20K 20K) C. Both are the same size D. Neither is feasible
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: B. A 20K 20K matrix has 400 million entries. A 300M 300M matrix has 90 quadrillion . At scale, item-item collaborative filtering is far more practical.
# ---
Exactly, it's B. Twenty thousand times twenty thousand is about 400 million entries, which is big but manageable, while 300 million squared is around 90 quadrillion. That's why at real-world scale, comparing items instead of users is so much more practical.
