# ON SCREEN:
# Quick Check: Why do we normalise per person before comparing tastes? A. To make the matrix square B. To strip the rating scale so only the preference pattern remains C. To remove NaN values D. To sort the movies alphabetically
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: B. A harsh critic s 3 and a generous critic s 5 can mean the same thing. Normalising removes the scale and reveals the pattern.
# ---
That's right, it's B! A harsh critic's 3 and a generous critic's 5 might mean the very same thing, "I liked this one." Once we normalise, the scale melts away and what's left is each person's real pattern of taste.
