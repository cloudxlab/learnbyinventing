# ON SCREEN:
# Each person is now a column of 25 numbers (one per movie). Two people with similar taste will have similar columns. How do we measure similar direction ?
# [Fragment 0]: Cosine similarity: [math] Result is between 1 (opposite) and +1 (identical direction). It ignores magnitude only direction matters.
# ---
Cosine similarity gives us exactly that: it compares the direction two columns point in, and scores them from minus one for opposite taste to plus one for identical. Since it ignores magnitude, someone who rates everything high and someone who rates everything low can still count as a match if their pattern is the same.
