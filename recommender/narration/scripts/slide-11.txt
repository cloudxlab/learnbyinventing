# Your Taste Twin Your task: write returning the name of the person with the highest similarity to -- excluding the person themselves (everyone's best match would otherwise be themselves, similarity 1.0).
# ---
Here's a fun one: who in this class has the taste most like yours? You already built a way to measure how similar two people are. Now use it to find your closest match, your taste twin.

There's one catch. If you compare yourself to everyone, you'll match yourself perfectly. So think about how to leave yourself out. Give it a try before moving on.
