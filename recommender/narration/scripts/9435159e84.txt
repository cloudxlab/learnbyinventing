# ON SCREEN:
# Now the core: score each candidate movie. The score is a weighted sum people who are more similar to you contribute more to the score of each movie.
# ---
Now we get to the heart of the recommender. For each movie you haven't seen, we need a score, and the idea is a weighted sum. Every person who rated that movie gets a vote, but not all votes count equally. People who are more similar to you pull harder on the score. Think about what that means for a moment before we move on.
