You've just built something real: collaborative filtering. Notice that your recommender never knew a single thing about the movies. No genres, no actors, no plots. It only looked at the pattern of who likes what. The alternative, content-based filtering, compares movie features instead, and modern systems blend both. Here's the fun part: that all-pairs similarity trick, one matrix product, shows up again inside transformers. It's the heart of attention, where every word compares itself to every other word. Same math, different vectors.
