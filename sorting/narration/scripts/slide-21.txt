Quick sort has a lovely trick. Once you've partitioned, the pivot is already sitting exactly where it belongs, and what's left is just two smaller messy regions, one on each side. So how would you sort those? The same way you just did. Try writing it so it sorts in place, no new lists. And think about this: merge sort needed extra memory. How much does quick sort need?
