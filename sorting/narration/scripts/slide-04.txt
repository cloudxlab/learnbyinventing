# Part 1: Comparing Neighbors The idea: walk through the list comparing neighbors . Whenever the left neighbor is bigger, swap them. What happens to the largest value after one full pass?
# ---
Here's a simple idea to start with: walk through the list, and look at two neighbors at a time. If the one on the left is bigger than the one on its right, swap them. Before you run anything, picture it on a small list. Where does the biggest number end up after one full pass? Try it with pencil and paper first, then see if your guess holds.
