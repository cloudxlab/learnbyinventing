Now let's measure the work. Modify your bubble sort to count every comparison it makes, then run it on lists of length 10, 100, and 1000. Watch how the count grows. When n gets 10 times bigger, the comparisons don't just grow 10 times. They grow about 100 times. That's the signature of quadratic time, and it's why we need faster ideas.
