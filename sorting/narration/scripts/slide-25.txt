Here's a question that gets at something important. Counting sort sorts a billion ages in linear time, which is faster than merge sort. So why doesn't everyone use it for everything?

Think back to how you built it. What did you need to know before you could even start? And what would happen if your values weren't small whole numbers, like ages, but arbitrary decimals?

Read through the four options, think about your own counting sort, and pick one before you move on.
