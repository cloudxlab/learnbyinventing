We've built merge sort, and it works well. But look at how it behaves. It splits the list blindly down the middle, without even looking at the values. Then all the real work happens later, while merging. So here's a question to think about. What if we did the work while splitting instead? If the pieces were already arranged the right way, would we still need to merge at all?
