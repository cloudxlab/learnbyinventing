# ON SCREEN:
# Exercise: write insert_card(lst, k) that moves lst[k] left into its correct position in the sorted prefix.
# ---
Think about picking up a playing card. The cards already in your hand are sorted. You take the new card and slide it left until it's in the right spot. That's exactly what insert_card does: starting at position k, swap left one step at a time until the element is where it belongs.
