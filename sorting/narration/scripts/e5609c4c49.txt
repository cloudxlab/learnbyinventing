# Part 2: Sorting Like Playing Cards Think about how you sort playing cards in your hand: you pick up cards one at a time, and insert each new card into its correct place among the cards you already hold — which are always sorted.
# ---
Now let's try something different. Think about how you sort playing cards in your hand. You pick up one card at a time, and slide it into the right spot among the cards you're already holding. Those cards are always in order. Notice how natural that feels. Keep that picture in mind.
