# ---
You've seen how to split a list into zeros and ones. Now let's make that idea bigger. Instead of two fixed values, pick one element from the list, called the pivot, and rearrange everything so the smaller items sit on its left and the larger ones on its right.

Your job is to write this as a function that works in place and returns the index where the pivot ends up. Think about how you did the zeros and ones, and give it a try yourself before moving on.
