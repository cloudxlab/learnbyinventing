# Insert Into a Sorted Prefix Suppose is already sorted and is the new card. Write that moves left, one swap at a time, until it sits in its correct position. In place. [code block]
# ---
Here's a card game problem. Imagine you're holding a hand of cards that's already in order, and someone hands you one new card. Your job is to slide it into the right spot. Think about it: how does the new card know when to stop moving? And what does each swap actually do? Try writing it yourself, one swap at a time, without building a whole new list. Take your time, then we'll check it together.
