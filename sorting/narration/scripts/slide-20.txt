Here's a thought: after partitioning, one element is already exactly where it belongs. The pivot is done. So what's left? Just two smaller messy regions, one on each side. And here's the fun part: you already know how to sort a messy region. Do the very same thing to each. Try writing it yourself, sorting in place. Then ask yourself: merge sort needed extra memory, so how much does quick sort need?
