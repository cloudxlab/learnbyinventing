# Exercise: write pivot_sort(lst, lo, hi) that sorts in place using partition and recursion.
# ---
After partitioning, the pivot sits in its final sorted position. Everything to its left is smaller, everything to its right is bigger. Now just recursively sort those two sides the same way. Notice that unlike merge sort, you don't need extra memory for merging. The work happens during the split, not the combine.
