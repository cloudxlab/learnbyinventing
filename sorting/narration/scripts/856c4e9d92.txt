# Part 5: Sorting Without Comparing A puzzle: You must sort the ages of a billion people . Ages are whole numbers between 0 and 200. Your Part 3 algorithm would need ~30 billion comparisons. Can you sort them with zero comparisons between elements ? Hint: how many different values can an age take?
# ---
Here's a puzzle that might feel impossible at first. Imagine you have to sort the ages of a billion people. Our best comparison-based sort would need around thirty billion comparisons. But what if you didn't compare any two people at all? Before you move on, think about the hint: how many different ages can there really be?
