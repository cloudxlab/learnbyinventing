Sorting looks like a problem that's already solved. You just call the built-in function and move on. But almost every big idea in algorithm design shows up first in sorting: nested loops, invariants, divide and conquer, even trading memory for speed.

In this chapter, you'll invent five different sorting algorithms yourself. Then we'll race them against each other and find out why some are dramatically faster. We'll even see when you can beat all of them. Ready? Let's dive in.
