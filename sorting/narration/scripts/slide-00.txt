Sorting looks like a solved problem, right? You just call the built-in function and move on. But here's the secret: almost every big idea in algorithm design shows up first in sorting. Nested loops, invariants, divide and conquer, even trading memory for speed. In this chapter, you'll invent five different sorting algorithms yourself, race them against each other, and discover why some are dramatically faster. You might even find out when you can beat them all.
