# ON SCREEN:
# There could be a complex relationship between inputs and outputs: y = a x&sup2; + b x + c
# ---
We've been fitting straight lines this whole time. But the real world isn't always that simple. Sometimes the relationship between inputs and outputs bends and curves. Here's one possibility: y equals a times x squared, plus b times x, plus c. Take a moment and think about how this differs from our line.
