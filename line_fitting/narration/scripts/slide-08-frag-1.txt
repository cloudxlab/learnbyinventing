# Do you know how an electronic thermometer works? When a metal is heated, its resistance changes and that changes the electric current through it. We can measure the current and estimate the temperature from it.
# [Fragment 1]: Put the two values in the equation. You get two equations: 10 = a 1 + b (1) 50 = a 1.2 + b (2)
# ---
Put those numbers into the equation and each reading gives us its own equation. A current of one gives a temperature of ten, and a current of one point two gives fifty. Now we've got two equations and two unknowns, so take a moment and think about how you'd solve for a and b.
