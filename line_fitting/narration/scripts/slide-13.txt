# ON SCREEN:
# The best fit line is the one with the lowest MSE . Finding it efficiently is what gradient descent does.
# ---
We've seen that every line we try has an error score, the MSE, and some lines score better than others. So here's the big idea: the best fit line is simply the one with the lowest MSE. The tricky part is finding it efficiently, without trying every possible line. That's exactly what gradient descent does, and it's what we'll invent next.
