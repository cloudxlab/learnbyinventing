Here's a thought: real problems rarely have just one input. What if we're predicting something using two inputs, like x one and x two? Then our line grows a second ingredient, and the prediction becomes a weighted mix of both inputs, plus a constant.

Take a moment to think about what each of those weights is really doing. Does it feel familiar?
