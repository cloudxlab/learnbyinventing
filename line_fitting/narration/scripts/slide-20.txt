So far we've fitted lines with one input, but real problems rarely stop there. What if the thing you're predicting depends on two inputs, or even ten? Think about predicting a house price from both its size and its number of bedrooms. Here, each input gets its own weight, plus one shared starting value. Take a moment to think about what we'd need to fit now.
