Here's your exercise. Now that you understand what mean squared error is, write a function that computes it. Given a slope a, an intercept b, and lists of x and y values, your function should return the average of the squared errors. Test it with the line three x minus one against our three data points. You should get roughly zero point three three. Try it yourself first, and use the hints if you need them.
