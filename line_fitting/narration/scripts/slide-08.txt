# Do you know how an electronic thermometer works? When a metal is heated, its resistance changes and that changes the electric current through it. We can measure the current and estimate the temperature from it.
# ---
Ever wondered how an electronic thermometer actually works? Here's the trick: when a metal heats up, its electrical resistance changes, and that changes how much current flows through it. So the device never measures temperature directly. It measures current, and then estimates the temperature from that. Let's think about how that estimate might work.
