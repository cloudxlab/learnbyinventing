Have you ever wondered how an electronic thermometer actually works? Here's the trick: when a piece of metal heats up, its resistance changes, and that changes how much electric current flows through it. So the device never measures temperature directly. It measures current, then works out the temperature from that. Keep that idea in mind, because it's going to matter in a moment.
