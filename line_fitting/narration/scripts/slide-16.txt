# The best fit line is the one with the lowest MSE . Finding it efficiently is what gradient descent does.
# ---
We've been circling one idea this whole time: the best line is the one with the lowest error. Every slope and intercept we tried had its own MSE, and the winner was simply the smallest. But trying every possibility would take forever. So next, we'll invent a smarter way to find that lowest point: gradient descent.
