# ON SCREEN:
# The best fit line is the one with the lowest MSE . Finding it efficiently is what gradient descent does.
# ---
Here's the payoff of everything you've built so far. You now have a way to score any line: the mean squared error. Lower means a better fit. So the best line is simply the one with the lowest score. But trying every possible slope and intercept would take forever. Next, we'll see how to walk downhill toward the best line efficiently. That's the big idea behind gradient descent.
