# ON SCREEN:
# Not all patterns are linear. Consider this data: x y 0 0 1 1 2 4 3 9
# ---
Not every pattern is a straight line, and this slide shows why. Take a look at these four points. When x is zero, y is zero. When x is one, y is one. Then four, then nine.

Before we go on, try this yourself. Can a single straight line pass through all of them? Look at how fast y is growing from one step to the next, and see what you notice.
