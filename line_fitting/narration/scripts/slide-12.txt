# Part 2: What If the Data Doesn't Fit Perfectly?
# ---
So far, we've found a line that passes perfectly through every point. But real data is rarely that tidy. Measurements are noisy, and points scatter around a trend instead of sitting on it. So here's the question to hold onto: if no single line can hit every point, how do we decide which line is the best one? Let's find out.
