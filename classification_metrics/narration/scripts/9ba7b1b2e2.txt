# What you just invented: mapping table from learner concepts to standard ML names
# ---
Look at this table. Counting correct and wrong predictions by type? That's the confusion matrix. Asking what fraction of your positive predictions were right? That's precision. Asking how many real positives you caught? That's recall. The score that stays low unless both are high? That's the F1 score, a harmonic mean. Converting probabilities to zeros and ones with a cutoff? Thresholding. Plotting TPR versus FPR? The ROC curve. And summing trapezoids under it? That's AUC, the area under the curve.
