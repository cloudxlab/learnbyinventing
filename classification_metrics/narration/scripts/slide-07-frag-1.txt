# Now that we have TP=4, TN=4, FP=1, FN=1 what should we measure? The model made 5 positive predictions (TP + FP). What fraction of those were actually positive?
# [Fragment 1]: 4 / 5 = 0.80 This is Recall = TP / (TP + FN) Of all the real positives, how many did I find?
# ---
Here's the catch: that 4 out of 5 is actually precision. It answers a different question from recall, which asks about all the real positives and how many we found. The question on this slide asks what fraction of our positive predictions were right, so the formula shown here doesn't match it.

Try it yourself: say what 4 out of 5 tells us about our predictions, then work out which fraction would measure the real positives we caught.
