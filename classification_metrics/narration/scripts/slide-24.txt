# ON SCREEN:
# Part 4: ROC Curve & AUC
# ---
So far we've asked whether a model got each prediction right or wrong. But there's a hidden knob we haven't looked at yet: the threshold. Most models actually output a score, and we decide where to draw the line between yes and no. In this part, we'll see what happens as we slide that line and how to summarize the whole story in one number.
