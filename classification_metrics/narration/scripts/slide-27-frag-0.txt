# What is the area under the line connecting these two points? (5, 6) and (7, 10)
# [Fragment 0]: This is a trapezoid ! Area = width average height = (7 5) (6 + 10) / 2 = 2 8 = 16
# ---
Think of it as a trapezoid: the width is two, and the average of the two heights is eight. Multiply them and you get sixteen. Hold onto that trick, because we're about to use it on every little slice under a curve.
