Here's a puzzle that might change how you think about accuracy. A hospital tests a thousand patients for a rare disease, and only ten of them are actually sick. Now imagine a lazy model that just says "nobody is sick" every single time. Before you move on, work out its accuracy yourself. What number do you get?
