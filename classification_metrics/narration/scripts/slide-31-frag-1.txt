# The Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC) summarizes model quality in a single number. A perfect model s curve covers the entire square. What is its AUC? A random model follows the diagonal. What area is that?
# [Fragment 1]: [code block] The higher the AUC, the better the model separates the two classes regardless of which threshold you pick.
# ---
That's the big idea: one number that tells you how well your model separates the two classes, no matter where you set the threshold. The closer you get to one, the better your model is.
