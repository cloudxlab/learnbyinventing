Every prediction your model makes has two properties. First, what was the actual label, zero or one? Second, what did the model predict, zero or one? Two choices times two choices gives four possible outcomes. Here's your challenge: try arranging those four outcomes in a table, with actual labels as rows and predictions as columns. Give it a go before moving on.
