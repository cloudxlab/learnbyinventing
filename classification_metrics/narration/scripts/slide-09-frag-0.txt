# ON SCREEN:
# Can we combine precision and recall into one number ?
# [Fragment 0]: F1 Score = 2PR / (P + R) the harmonic mean, not (P+R)/2, because the arithmetic mean lets one bad metric hide behind a good one (e.g. P=1.0, R=0.0 average 0.5 but F1 = 0)
# ---
Here's a handy trick: just averaging the two can be misleading. Say precision is perfect but recall is zero. The plain average still says fifty percent, which sounds okay. The F1 score uses the harmonic mean, and it drops that to zero, so one bad metric can't hide behind a good one.
