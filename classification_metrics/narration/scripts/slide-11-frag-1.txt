# Accuracy looks great at 0.80. But consider this: A hospital tests 1000 patients for a rare disease. Only 10 actually have it. A lazy model says: Nobody is sick. What is its accuracy?

Zero true positives and ten missed patients, so recall is 0%. The model never caught a single sick person, yet its accuracy is 99%. That's why accuracy can fool us when one class is rare, and why we need precision and recall.
