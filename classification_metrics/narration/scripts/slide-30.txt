# ON SCREEN:
# The Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC) summarizes model quality in a single number. A perfect model s curve covers the entire square. What is its AUC? A random model follows the diagonal. What area is that?
# ---
Now let's put a single number on how good a model is. That's what the area under the ROC curve does. Picture a perfect model, one whose curve hugs the top-left corner and covers the whole square. What would its area be? Then think about the random model sitting on the diagonal. What area does that leave underneath? Try working both out yourself first.
