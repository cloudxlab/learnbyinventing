# [Fragment 1]: A few more metrics: Accuracy, TPR, FPR
# ---
Here are a few more metrics you'll want in your toolkit. Accuracy is just total correct divided by total predictions, but be careful with it. TPR, the true positive rate, is actually the same as recall, and it'll come back when we build ROC curves. FPR, the false positive rate, asks: of all the negatives, how many did the model incorrectly flag as positive?
