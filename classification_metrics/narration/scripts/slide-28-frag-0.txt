# ON SCREEN:
# What is the area under the line connecting these two points? (5, 6) and (7, 10)
# [Fragment 0]: This is a trapezoid ! Area = width average height = (7 5) (6 + 10) / 2 = 2 8 = 16
# ---
That's a trapezoid. Its area is the width, which is two, times the average of the two heights, which is eight. So the area comes out to sixteen. Keep that trick in mind, because we're about to use it again and again.
