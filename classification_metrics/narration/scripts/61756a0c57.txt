A perfect model covers the entire unit square, so its AUC is one point zero. A random model traces the diagonal, giving a triangle with area zero point five. If a model's AUC is below zero point five, it's actually worse than flipping a coin. Now, you already have your area-under-curve function. Your next task is to apply it to the ROC points by writing a compute_auc function.
