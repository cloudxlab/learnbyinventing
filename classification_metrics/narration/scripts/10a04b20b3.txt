# The Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC) summarizes model quality in a single number. A perfect model s curve covers the entire square. What is its AUC? A random model follows the diagonal. What area is that?
# ---
Now let's put a number on how good a model is. The area under the ROC curve, or AUC, boils all that curve-reading into one score. Picture a perfect model, whose curve fills the whole square. What would its area be? Then think about the random diagonal. What area does that leave? Give it a try before moving on.
