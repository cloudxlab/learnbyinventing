# ON SCREEN:
# Can we combine precision and recall into one number ?
# [Fragment 0]: F1 Score = 2PR / (P + R) the harmonic mean, not (P+R)/2, because the arithmetic mean lets one bad metric hide behind a good one (e.g. P=1.0, R=0.0 average 0.5 but F1 = 0)
# ---
Here's a trick for combining them: the F1 score. Why not just average precision and recall? Because a model with perfect precision and zero recall would still score a respectable fifty percent, which hides a total failure. The harmonic mean punishes that kind of imbalance, so F1 drops all the way to zero.
