# ON SCREEN:
# Reflection questions about accuracy, precision, AUC, trapezoid rule, and fraud detection
# ---
Take a moment to think about these questions in your own words. Why does accuracy fail when classes are lopsided? If someone brags about perfect precision, what might they be hiding? Can you explain AUC without saying area, curve, or ROC? Where else could you use the trapezoid rule? And for fraud detection, would you lean toward precision or recall? There's no single right answer to these, but thinking them through will solidify everything you've built today.
