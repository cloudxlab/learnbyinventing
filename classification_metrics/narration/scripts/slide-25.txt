# Part 4: ROC Curve & AUC
# ---
We've covered a lot of ground with single-number metrics like precision, recall, and F1. But they all depend on one choice: where you set the threshold. So what if we could see how a model behaves at every threshold at once? That's the idea behind the ROC curve and AUC, and you're about to invent it.
