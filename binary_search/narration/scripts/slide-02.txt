# Guess My Number Suppose someone thinks of an integer between 1 and 1000 (inclusive). You can ask "Is it less than, equal to, or greater than X?" and they will answer honestly. Question 1: If you always guess randomly, how many guesses might you need in the worst case? Question 2: If you always guess the middle of the remaining range, how many guesses do you need in the worst case? Try it on paper for the range [1, 1000]. Key insight: Each "guess the middle" halves the range. After k guesses the range has shrunk from 1000 to roughly [math]. How many times can you halve 1000 before you reach 1? [code block] Write a function that returns how many times you can halve (starting from a whole number) before reaching 1 or below. [code block]

Let's start with a classic game. Someone's thinking of a whole number between one and a thousand, and you get to ask whether it's higher or lower than your guess. How would you find it fastest? Grab a pencil and compare two strategies: guessing randomly, and always guessing the middle. Each middle guess cuts the possibilities in half, so here's the real question: how many times can you halve a thousand before you hit one? Try writing that as a function yourself.
