# ON SCREEN:
# Binary Search Complexity A sorted list has 1,000,000 elements. What is the maximum number of comparisons binary search needs to find an element (or confirm it's missing)? A. 1,000,000 B. 1,000 C. About 20 D. About 500,000
# ---
Here's a question to stretch your intuition. We've got a sorted list with a million elements. Think back to how each step of binary search throws away half of what's left. Before you peek at the options, try to work out roughly how many times you can cut a million in half before only one item remains. Take a moment, then pick your answer.
