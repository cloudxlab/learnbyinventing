Now that you can find roots, let's try something that feels like the reverse. Instead of asking "what number, multiplied by itself a few times, gives this?", we're asking "what exponent do I need to hit a target?" You already know the search itself. The new puzzle is where to start looking, since the answer could be any real number. How would you pick a bracket? Think about it for a moment.
