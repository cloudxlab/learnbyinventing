Now let's put it all together on a real list. Imagine a sorted list and a number you're hunting for. Your job is to write a function that answers one question: is it in there?

Peek at the middle element first. Is your target smaller or bigger? That tells you which half is worth searching. Then repeat on that half.

No walking the whole list. For a million items, you should only need about twenty looks. Give it a try yourself.
