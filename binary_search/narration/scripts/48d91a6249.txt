Welcome to a bonus section! So far we've been searching through a smooth, continuous range of numbers. But the same halving trick works on a sorted list, and this is the version you'll run into everywhere, from databases to Python's standard library. Two things change. Your low and high are now positions in the list, not values. And instead of stopping at a precision, you stop when those positions cross.
