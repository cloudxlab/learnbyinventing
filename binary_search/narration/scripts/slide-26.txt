You've just invented something really powerful. Whenever the answer sits somewhere in a range, and you can ask "too high or too low?", you can cut the range in half, again and again. That's how you found square roots, cube roots, and logarithms, and even searched sorted lists. Take a moment to look over what you built, because this same halving idea shows up everywhere, from gradient descent to decision trees.
