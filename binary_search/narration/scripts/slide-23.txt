Here's one last twist, and it's a fun one. This array isn't sorted. It goes down, then comes back up, like a valley. Can binary search still work? It can. Look at the middle element and the one right next to it. If the numbers are still falling, you're on the downhill slope, so the bottom must be further right. Otherwise, it's at or to the left. Give it a try yourself. And remember this idea of walking downhill until you can't go lower. It's exactly how gradient descent trains machine-learning models, and you'll meet it again soon.
