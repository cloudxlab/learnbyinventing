# Binary Search Complexity A sorted list has 1,000,000 elements. What is the maximum number of comparisons binary search needs to find an element (or confirm it's missing)? A. 1,000,000 B. 1,000 C. About 20 D. About 500,000
# ---
Here's a question to test what you've just discovered. Picture a sorted list with a million elements. Before you look at the options, think back to what happens each time we compare: we throw away half of what's left. So how many times could you cut a million in half before only one item remains? Have a go yourself, then pick your answer.
