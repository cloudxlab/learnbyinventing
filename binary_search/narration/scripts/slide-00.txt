Welcome! Here's the one big idea behind this whole chapter: if you know something only ever goes up, or only ever goes down, you can find any target value inside it by cutting your search range in half, again and again. We'll use that single trick to approximate square roots, cube roots, and even logarithms, without any built-in shortcuts. Ready to discover how?
