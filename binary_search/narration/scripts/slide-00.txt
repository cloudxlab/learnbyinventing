Welcome! This chapter is built around one surprisingly powerful idea. If a function only ever goes up, or only ever goes down, you can find any target value in it by cutting your search range in half, again and again. That's the whole trick. Along the way, you'll use it to approximate square roots, cube roots, and even logarithms, without calling any built-in functions. Ready to invent it yourself?
