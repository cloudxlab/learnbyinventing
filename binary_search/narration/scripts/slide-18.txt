# ---
So far, everything we've searched has been a smooth range of numbers, like finding a square root between zero and some big value. But the same halving trick works on a sorted list too, and that's the version you'll meet everywhere, from databases to Python's standard library. Just two things change. Your low and high are now positions in the list, not numbers. And instead of stopping at some precision, you stop when those positions cross each other.
