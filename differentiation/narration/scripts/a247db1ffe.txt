That ratio you just built has a proper name: it's called the derivative. It tells us how fast one quantity changes with respect to another. You'll see it written in a couple of different ways, and don't worry, they all mean the same thing. And the process of finding it? That's called differentiation. So you've already been differentiating without knowing it.
