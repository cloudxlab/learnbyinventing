# Part 9: The Second Derivative
# ---
We've covered a lot of ground, and now we get to the final big idea. You've seen that the derivative tells you how fast something is changing. But here's a fun question: what if we take the derivative of the derivative? That's called the second derivative, and it's about how the *rate of change itself* is changing. Let's see what it can tell us.
