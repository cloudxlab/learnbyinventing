So here's the big picture. When the slope is zero and it's heading from negative to positive, you've landed at the bottom of a bowl, a minimum. When the slope is zero and it's heading from positive to negative, you're at the top of a hill, a maximum. Notice how the slope's own direction of change tells you which one it is.
