# So far, your functions had one input . But what if a function takes two ?
# [Fragment 1]: The trick: differentiate with respect to one input at a time , treating the other as a constant. This is called a partial derivative .
# ---
Here's the trick: pick one input, differentiate with respect to just that one, and pretend the other is a fixed number. That's called a partial derivative. You already know how to do this, because a constant just sits there while the other input changes.
