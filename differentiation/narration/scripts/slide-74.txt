You just invented a lot of calculus, and you did it without being handed a single rule. Look at what you built. Nudging an input and measuring the change was numerical differentiation. Expanding, cancelling, and dropping the leftovers was the algebraic version, and out of it came the power rule. Nudging one input at a time gave you partial derivatives, and a list of those is the gradient. Let's see what else you discovered.
