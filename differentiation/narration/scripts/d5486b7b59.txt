# The rate of change of the slope is just the derivative of the derivative the second derivative : [math]
# ---
So far we've looked at how fast something changes, and that's the slope. But slopes can change too. Think about a car: its speed is how fast position changes, and how quickly the speed itself changes is acceleration. That's the derivative of the derivative, and we call it the second derivative.
