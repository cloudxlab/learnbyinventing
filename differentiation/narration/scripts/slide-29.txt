Here's one for you to try on your own. You've got a function with more than one variable, and the trick is to pick one variable at a time. Treat the other one as if it's just a fixed number, then differentiate with respect to the variable you've chosen.

Before you look at the answers, work through both parts with pencil and paper. Ask yourself what stays put and what changes. When you're ready, check your work.
