Here's the idea we just built from scratch: take the change in the output, divide it by the change in the input, and shrink that input change down toward nothing. That ratio has a name. It's called the derivative. You'll see it written a couple of different ways, and don't worry, they all mean the same thing. And the whole process of finding it? That's called differentiation. So you've been differentiating this whole time.
