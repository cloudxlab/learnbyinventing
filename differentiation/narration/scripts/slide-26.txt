# Part 4: What If There Are Two Inputs?
# ---
Welcome to Part 4. So far, everything we've worked with had just one input: you put in a single number, and you got a single number out. But real problems rarely work that way. Think about a game score that depends on both your speed and your accuracy. What do you think happens when a function has two inputs? Hold that question as we dive in.
