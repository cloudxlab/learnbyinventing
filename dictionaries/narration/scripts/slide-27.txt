Last time, we turned a single letter into a number using its character code. Now let's stretch that idea to a whole string. What's the simplest recipe you can think of? Add up the codes of every character, then take the remainder after dividing by the number of slots. Why does the remainder help? Because it always lands on a valid position. Try it on a few words yourself.
