# Dictionaries A dictionary maps keys to values — like an index at the back of a book. You look up a key in O(1) time, regardless of how many entries exist. In this chapter you will use dictionaries to: Count word frequencies in text Group words that are anagrams of each other Track and query expenses by category Navigate: press or Space to advance | to go back

Welcome! In this chapter, we're going to explore dictionaries, which are one of the most useful tools in Python. Think of a dictionary like the index at the back of a book: you look up a key, and it takes you straight to the answer, no matter how big the book is. Along the way, you'll count words, group anagrams, and track expenses. Ready to invent?
