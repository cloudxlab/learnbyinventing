Ever wonder why a dictionary lookup is instant, even with a million keys? A list would have to check entries one by one. So how does a dict jump straight to the right spot? Here's the big idea: turn the key into a number, then use that number as a position. Python gives you a tool for this. Your challenge is to write a function that returns a letter's position in the alphabet. Give it a try!
