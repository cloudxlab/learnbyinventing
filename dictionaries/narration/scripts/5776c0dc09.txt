# ON SCREEN:
# What You Just Invented
# ---
You just invented dictionaries, and you did it by solving real problems, not by memorizing a definition. You started with simple lookups. Then you counted words, grouped anagrams, and tracked expenses. Along the way you built your own hashing, and you saw why finding a key is so fast. Take a look at how your home-grown ideas match the standard names. You didn't just learn how to use a dictionary. You understand why it works.
