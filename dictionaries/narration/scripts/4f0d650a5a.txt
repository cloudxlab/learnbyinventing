Here's a fun twist. So far we've looked things up by key. Now imagine you want to go the other way, so the values become the keys and the keys become the values. Say you have names mapped to ages, and you want ages mapped to names.

Take a moment and think about how you'd build that new dictionary. What would you loop over? Give it a try before we move on.
