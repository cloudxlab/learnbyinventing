# Part 4: Invert a Dictionary & Group By Sometimes you need to look up a key by its value instead of the other way around. Inverting a dictionary swaps keys and values.
# ---
Sometimes you've got the answer and need to find the question. Say you have a dictionary mapping names to ages, but now you want to know who is a certain age. That means looking up a key by its value. Inverting a dictionary swaps the two roles. Before we start, think about what could go wrong when two people share the same age.
