We're starting a new part, and it's a fun one. You've already stored and counted things with dictionaries. Now let's flip them around. What if you had a dictionary, but you needed to look things up by the value instead of the key? And what if you wanted to gather everything that shares a common trait into groups? Give it some thought before we begin. You'll be inventing a small tool that handles both.
