You've built a whole toolkit here. Counting items, word frequencies, the most common word, anagram groups, spending totals, and even swapping keys and values. Look at them side by side and notice something: they're all the same move in different clothes. Each one sorts items into buckets by a key, then does something with each bucket. That's the one general idea hiding underneath all of them.
