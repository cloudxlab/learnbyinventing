# The pattern is one of the most frequently recurring patterns in data processing: collect items into buckets based on a computed property.
# ---
You've just built something you'll use again and again. Look back at what we did: we took a pile of items, worked out one property for each, and dropped every item into the bucket that matched. Anagrams, expenses, even hashing all followed this same shape. Once you spot it, you'll see it everywhere in data work.
