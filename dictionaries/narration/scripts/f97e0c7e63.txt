Look at everything you built and the real names they go by. Your count-items function is a frequency table, the same idea behind Python's Counter class. Your anagram key is called a canonical form. Group-by is the same operation that SQL databases call GROUP BY. Inverting a dictionary is building a reverse index. Your string hash is a hash function, and the weighted version is a polynomial rolling hash. The shard picker is hash-based partitioning. And the bucket-and-chain structure you invented? That's a hash table, the very thing that makes Python's dict work.
