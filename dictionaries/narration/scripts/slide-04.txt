Now that you know how to read from a dictionary, let's learn how to change one. Adding a new entry and updating an existing one use the very same move, so you'll see how simple that is. Start with an empty dict, add three entries one at a time, then bump one of the values by ten. Give it a try, and check that your dictionary has the right size and the right updated value.
