Here's a new kind of problem. You've got a list of expense entries, each one a category and an amount, and you want to keep a running total for every category in a dictionary. Before you code, think about two cases. What should happen the first time you see a category? And what should happen when it's already in there? Work out both on paper, then give it a try.
