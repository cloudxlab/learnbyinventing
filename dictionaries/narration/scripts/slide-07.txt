Now it's time to put your dictionary skills to work. Your task is to write a function that takes a sentence, splits it into words, and counts how often each one shows up. The result is a dictionary, with each word paired to its count.

Before you code, think about what happens when you see a word for the first time. What happens the second time? There's a hint here, too: you may already have written something earlier that does most of this work. Give it a try yourself first.
