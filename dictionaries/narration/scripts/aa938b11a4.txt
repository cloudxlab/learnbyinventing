# Quick Check: You used a dictionary to count words. Could you have used a list of pairs instead? What would be the main drawback? A. Lists can t store pairs B. It would work identically with no drawback C. Finding whether a word already exists would require scanning the entire list each time much slower for large texts D. Lists can t be sorted
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: C. With a list you d scan every pair to check if a word exists that gets slow as the text grows. A dictionary jumps straight to the right entry.
# ---
That's right, it's C! With a list of pairs, you'd have to check every single entry just to see if a word was already there, and that slows down fast as the text gets bigger. A dictionary skips the searching and jumps straight to the right entry.
