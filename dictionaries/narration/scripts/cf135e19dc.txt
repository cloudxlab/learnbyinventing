# listen and silent contain the exact same letters. So do evil , vile , live , and veil . How could you represent a word so that all its anagrams produce the same key?
# [Fragment 0]: Sort the letters! . . Same key.
# ---
Sort the letters of any anagram and they all collapse into the same string. Listen and silent both become "eilnst," and that shared sorted word is your key.
