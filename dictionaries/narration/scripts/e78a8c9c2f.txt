Here's a fun puzzle. Listen and silent are different words, but they use exactly the same letters. So do evil, vile, live, and veil. Words like these are called anagrams.

Now imagine you want to group them together, using a dictionary. A dictionary needs a key, and every anagram has to land on the same one. So how could you represent a word so that all its anagrams produce the identical key? Take a moment and think about it before we move on.
