# The pattern is one of the most frequently recurring patterns in data processing: collect items into buckets based on a computed property.

You've now built the same move several times: word counts, anagram groups, grouping by some property. Notice the shape. You take each item, compute something about it, and drop it into the bucket that matches. It's one of the most common patterns in data processing, and you just invented it yourself. Keep an eye out for it.
