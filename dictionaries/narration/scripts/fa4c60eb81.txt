Here's a problem you've actually solved before, just in a different costume. You've got a dictionary of word counts, and you want the word that shows up the most. So how do you find the key with the biggest value?

Before you write any code, think about it on paper. Imagine walking through the entries one by one, keeping track of just two things: the best word you've seen so far, and its count. Sound familiar? Give it a try yourself first.
