Now we're moving on to a fun puzzle: anagrams. Two words are anagrams if they use exactly the same letters, just shuffled around. "Listen" and "silent" are a pair, and so are "evil," "vile," "live," and "veil."

Here's the challenge. Can you find a way to represent any word so that all of its anagrams end up with the same key? Take a moment and think about it before we go on.
