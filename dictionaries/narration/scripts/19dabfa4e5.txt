You've used dictionaries all chapter, and you've probably noticed something almost magical. Looking up a key is instant, even with a million entries. A list would have to check them one by one. So how does a dict jump straight to the right spot? Here's the idea you're about to invent: turn the key into a number, then use that number as a position.
