# ---
Now we're moving on to a classic puzzle: anagrams. Two words are anagrams if they use exactly the same letters, just shuffled. "Listen" and "silent" are a pair, and so are "evil," "vile," "live," and "veil."

Here's the challenge. How could you turn a word into something, a key, so that every one of its anagrams ends up with the same one? Take a moment and think about what stays the same when you shuffle letters.
