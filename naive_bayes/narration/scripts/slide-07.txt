Time to get your hands dirty with the Play Tennis table. Just by counting rows, figure out how many of the nine Yes days have a Sunny outlook. Then do the same for the five No days.

Once you have those two counts, turn each into a fraction. Ask yourself, "Among Yes days, what share are Sunny?" and then the same question for No days.

Take a moment and work it out yourself before moving on. Notice how we're only looking at one group of days at a time. That's the key idea.
