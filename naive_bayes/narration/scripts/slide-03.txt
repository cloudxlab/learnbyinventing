Before we build anything fancy, let's start with the simplest situation: you know nothing about the weather. A friend calls and asks, should I come play tennis? What would you say, and why? Look back at our fourteen days of data and work out the fraction of days that were Yes and the fraction that were No. Then ask yourself: why must those two fractions add up to one? Give it a try yourself before moving on.
