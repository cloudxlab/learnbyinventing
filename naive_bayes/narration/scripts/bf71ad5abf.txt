# Fix It: Laplace Smoothing — pretend you've seen every combination at least once...
# ---
Here is the fix, and it is beautifully simple. Instead of getting a zero when you have never seen a combination, you pretend you saw it once. Add one to every numerator, and add k to every denominator, where k is the number of possible values for that feature. This keeps zero from ever appearing in a product, without distorting the real counts too much. Now write the probability_smooth function. The hint and solution are below if you need them.
