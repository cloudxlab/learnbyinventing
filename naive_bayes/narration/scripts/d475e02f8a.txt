The answer is B. We assume that the features are conditionally independent given the class label. That means knowing the outlook does not tell you anything extra about the temperature, once you already know whether we played. This assumption is almost never perfectly true in real data, but the classifier still works surprisingly well. And that assumption is exactly why it is called "naive" Bayes.
