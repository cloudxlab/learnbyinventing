To convert scores to probabilities, divide each score by the sum of all scores: For example, if Score(Yes) = 0.03 and Score(No) = 0.07, then: P(Yes) = 0.03 / (0.03 + 0.07) = 0.3 P(No) = 0.07 / (0.03 + 0.07) = 0.7 [code block] Test: The probabilities for each row should sum to 1.0.

Right now our model only tells us the winning label, which throws away a lot of useful information. Let's fix that by returning a probability for every class. Here's the trick: take each class's score and divide it by the sum of all the scores. So a score of 0.03 for Yes and 0.07 for No becomes thirty percent and seventy percent.
