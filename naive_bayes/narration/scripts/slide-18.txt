# Three Features by Hand It's Sunny , Hot , and High humidity. Compute Score(Yes) and Score(No) using all three features. You already have P(Sunny|Yes) and P(Hot|Yes). Now find P(High|Yes) and P(High|No) from the data. Score(Yes) = P(Sunny|Yes) × P(Hot|Yes) × P(High|Yes) × P(Yes) Score(No) = P(Sunny|No) × P(Hot|No) × P(High|No) × P(No) Which class wins?

You've already got the sunny and hot pieces for each class, so the new job is finding how often high humidity shows up among the Yes days and among the No days. Multiply all four numbers for each class, then compare the two scores. Give it a go yourself, then see which class wins.
