Here's a problem with measuring how well our line fits. For each point, the line makes a prediction, and the error is how far off that prediction is from the real value. But look closely: some errors are positive and some are negative, so if we just add them up, they can cancel each other out. A terrible line might look perfect. Pause here and think: how could you make every error positive before adding them? See if you can come up with at least one way.
