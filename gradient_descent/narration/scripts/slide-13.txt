# At any point [math] on the curve, the slope tells you: Slope positive (uphill to the right) move left Slope negative (uphill to the left) move right Slope zero you re at the bottom !
# ---
Here's a neat trick: at any point on the curve, just look at the slope. If it's positive, the curve is climbing to the right, so the bottom must be to your left. If it's negative, the curve is climbing to the left, so you head right. Notice the pattern? The slope is telling you which way to walk.
