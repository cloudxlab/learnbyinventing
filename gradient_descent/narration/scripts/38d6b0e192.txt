# Your turn: Write a formula that squares each error and takes the mean.
# ---
Your turn. We've been looking at errors, the gap between what our line predicts and what actually happened. Now let's turn that into a single number that tells us how bad the line is.

Here's the idea. Take each error and square it, so every miss becomes positive and big misses count extra. Then take the average of all those squared errors.

Try writing that as a formula or a small function on your own. Give it a real go before you look at anything else.
