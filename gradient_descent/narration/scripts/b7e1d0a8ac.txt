Notice the shape of this curve. It looks like a bowl, doesn't it? That bowl shape has a name in math: we call it convex.

A function is convex if it curves upward everywhere. Here's a simple way to picture it. Pick any two points on the curve and draw a straight line between them. The curve will always stay below that line.

Try that with a few pairs of points in your head. It's a handy test.
