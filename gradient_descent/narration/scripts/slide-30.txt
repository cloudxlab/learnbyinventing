# ON SCREEN:
# MSE is a function of [math] and [math]. What shape is its landscape?
# ---
Here's the setup. Our error measure, the mean squared error, depends on two numbers at once: the slope and the intercept of our line. So if we picture every possible pair of values, each one has its own error, and together they form a landscape.

Before we move on, pause and imagine it. What shape do you think that landscape has? A bowl, a ridge, something bumpy? Make a guess, then let's see.
