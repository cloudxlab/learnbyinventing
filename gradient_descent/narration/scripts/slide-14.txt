# At any point [math] on the curve, the slope tells you: Slope positive (uphill to the right) move left Slope negative (uphill to the left) move right Slope zero you re at the bottom !
# ---
Here's a thought: when you're standing somewhere on this curve, you don't need to see the whole thing. You just need the slope right where you are. So let's think about what that slope is telling you.

If the slope is positive, the curve is going uphill to the right, so the bottom must be the other way. Move left. If it's negative, move right. Pause for a second and picture it for yourself.
