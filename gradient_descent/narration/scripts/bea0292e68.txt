Your strategy works, and that's a real win. But did you notice how slow it feels? You're taking lots of tiny, careful steps, just checking left against right every time. So here's a question to think about: what if you could know exactly which way is downhill, and how steep it is, without having to poke around? That's what we call the slope, and it's what we'll invent next.
