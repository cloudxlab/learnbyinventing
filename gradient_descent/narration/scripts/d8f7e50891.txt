Now that we have a line and some data points, let's figure out how good that line really is. For each point, the line makes a prediction, and the gap between the prediction and the real value is the error. But here's a catch: some errors are positive and some are negative, so if you just add them up, they can cancel each other out. Pause here and think. How would you fix that? Try to come up with at least one way to make every error positive before adding.
