# ON SCREEN:
# MSE is a function of [math] and [math]. What shape is its landscape?
# ---
Until now we've been adjusting one number at a time. But mean squared error depends on two things at once: the slope and the intercept. So here's a question to think about. If you plotted the error for every possible pair of values, what shape would that landscape take? Take a moment and picture it before we continue.
