Time to try this one yourself. Picture standing on a hill, and all you can feel is which way the ground tilts. If the slope is positive, the ground rises to your right, so which way should you step to go downhill? And if it's negative, which way now?

Work that out on paper first. Then write the simplest version you can: check the slope, and move one fixed step in the right direction. Repeat until the slope is close to zero. Give it a real go before you look at any hints.
