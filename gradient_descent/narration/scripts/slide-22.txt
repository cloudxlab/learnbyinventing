You've probably landed on something like this already. When you worked out how the cost changes as you nudge each weight, one at a time, you ended up with a formula for each one. These are called partial derivatives. The name sounds fancy, but the idea is simple. You're measuring the slope in just one direction, while holding everything else still. Take a moment to compare these with what you wrote.
