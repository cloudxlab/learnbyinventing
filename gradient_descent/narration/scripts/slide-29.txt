Now that we've got a line and some data points, let's measure how wrong the line is. For each point, the line makes a prediction, and the error is how far off that prediction is. Here's the catch: some errors are positive, some are negative, and if you just add them up, they cancel out. So pause here and think. How could you make every error positive before adding? Try to come up with at least one way.
