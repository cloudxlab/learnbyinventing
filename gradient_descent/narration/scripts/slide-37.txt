So far, our line has handled everything we've thrown at it. But what if the data isn't straight? Here the targets are one, four, nine, sixteen, twenty-five. Those are squares, and they curve upward.

Go ahead and run the fit on this data yourself. Look at the error it gives you, and think about why the line can't get any better.
