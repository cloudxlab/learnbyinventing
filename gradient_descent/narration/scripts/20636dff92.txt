Take a look at the shape of that curve. It looks like a bowl, doesn't it? There's a name for that: we call a function convex when it curves upward everywhere. Here's a way to picture it. Pick any two points on the curve and draw a straight line between them. The curve always stays below that line. Keep that bowl in mind, because it's about to matter for finding the lowest point.
