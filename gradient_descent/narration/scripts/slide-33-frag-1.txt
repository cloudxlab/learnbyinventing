That closed-form formula only works because linear models are simple. Gradient descent doesn't care. It works on any differentiable function, even a neural network with millions of parameters. And the loop you just wrote, compute the gradient, take a step, repeat, is the same one that trains GPT, image classifiers, and AlphaFold.
