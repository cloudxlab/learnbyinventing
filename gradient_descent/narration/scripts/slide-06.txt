Notice the shape of this curve. It looks like a bowl, doesn't it? There's one clear low point, and the curve rises on both sides of it. Mathematicians have a name for this kind of shape: convex. A function is convex if it curves upward everywhere. Here's a way to picture it: pick any two points on the curve and draw a straight line between them. The curve always stays below that line.
