# ---
Here's a puzzle for you. We've got an error for every single point: what we predicted, minus what actually happened. Now, some of those errors come out positive, and some come out negative. If we just add them all up, they can cancel each other out, and a terrible model could look perfect. So how would you fix that? Pause here and think of at least one way to make every error positive before adding them. Try it yourself first!
