# ON SCREEN:
# Quick Check: What does the kernel detect? A. Vertical edges (left right changes) B. Horizontal edges (top bottom changes) C. Diagonal edges D. Nothing it blurs the image
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: B. Negative weights on top, positive on bottom. It responds to brightness differences between top and bottom rows horizontal edges.
# ---
Yes, B! With negative weights on top and positive weights on bottom, the kernel lights up wherever the brightness changes from the top row to the bottom row. That's exactly what a horizontal edge looks like.
