# Part 6: Upsampling to a Non-Integer Scale — 50x50 to 150x150
# ---
Here's a twist: so far we've scaled by whole numbers, like turning fifty by fifty into a hundred by hundred. Now we're going from fifty by fifty up to one hundred fifty by one hundred fifty. That's three times bigger, so it sounds easy. But think about it for a second. What does "in between" mean when the new pixels don't line up neatly with the old ones? Give it a try yourself before moving on.
