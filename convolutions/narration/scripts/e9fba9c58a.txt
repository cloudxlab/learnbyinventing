# Quick Check: Why does the standard formula give green the largest weight (0.587)? A. Green light has the most energy B. The human eye is most sensitive to green C. There are more green pixels in most images D. It s arbitrary any weights would work
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: B. Human vision has peak sensitivity in the green range. The formula (ITU-R BT.601) matches perceived brightness.
# ---
That's right, it's B. Our eyes are most sensitive to green light, so the formula gives green the biggest weight to match the brightness we actually perceive. If you picked something else, no worries, now you know why those numbers aren't arbitrary.
