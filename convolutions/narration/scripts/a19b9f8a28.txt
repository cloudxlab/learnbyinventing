# ON SCREEN:
# Before coding think on paper. You have two adjacent pixels with values 100 and 200 . You need to insert a new pixel between them. What value would look most natural?
# ---
Here's a puzzle before we write any code. Imagine two pixels sitting side by side, one with a brightness of one hundred and the other with two hundred. Now picture squeezing a brand-new pixel in between them.

What value would look most natural? Grab a pen and paper, and think about it for a moment. What would you pick, and why?
