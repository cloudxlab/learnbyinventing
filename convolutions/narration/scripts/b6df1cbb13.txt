# Downsampling shrinks. What about making an image bigger ? Simplest idea: repeat each pixel into a 2 2 block.
# ---
We've seen how downsampling shrinks an image. Now let's flip it around: what if we want to make an image bigger? Think about the simplest possible idea. Each pixel could just turn into a small two-by-two block of copies of itself. Picture what that would do to a tiny image, and what you'd expect the result to look like.
