# Bonus: Design a Filter by Intuition Here is a challenge with no given formula — you must invent the filter. Goal: Design a 3x3 kernel — experiment with different weight patterns and observe what each one does to the image. Think: You've seen kernels where all weights are equal, kernels with a large center weight and negative neighbors, and kernels with positive values on one side and negative on the other. What other patterns can you create? What happens if you put opposite signs on diagonal corners? What if only the corners have non-zero weights? Try at least 3 different kernels of your own design. Tasks: Design a 3x3 kernel, sketch it on paper, predict what it will do, then apply it to and check. Design a second kernel with a very different weight pattern. Apply and compare. Design a third kernel. Can you find one that highlights features the previous kernels missed? For each kernel, write down what you predicted vs. what you observed.

Here's a challenge with no formula to follow. This time, you're the filter designer. You've already seen kernels that blur, ones that sharpen, and ones that pick out edges. Now mix your own weight patterns and see what they do.

Before you run anything, sketch your kernel on paper and predict the result. What if opposite signs sit on the diagonal corners? What if only the corners have weights? Try at least three designs, and write down what you predicted next to what actually happened. Those surprises are where the real learning is. See if you can find one that catches something the others missed.
