# To a computer, an image is just a grid of numbers: [code block]
# ---
Here's a secret about pictures: a computer doesn't see them the way you do. When you look at a photo, you see faces, colors, shapes. But to the computer, an image is just a grid of numbers. Each number says how bright one tiny spot is. Take a look at this little example and notice how the numbers are laid out.
