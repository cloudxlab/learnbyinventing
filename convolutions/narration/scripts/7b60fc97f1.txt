# ON SCREEN:
# Quick Check: A 2 2 block contains pixels . What does average pooling produce? A. 20 median of the block B. 110 average of the four values C. 210 max of the block D. 10 min of the block
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: B. [math]. Average pooling blends all four pixels into one.
# ---
That's right, it's B! Average pooling blends all four pixels into one by adding them up and dividing by four, so you get a single value that sums up the whole block. Notice that it's not the biggest or smallest pixel, it's the middle ground of all of them.
