You've just built a whole toolkit, and it's time to see what it's really called. That grid of numbers from an image? It's a NumPy array, or an image tensor. Your weighted average of channels is the standard luminosity formula. Your pooling trick is average pooling, and your sliding window is a 2D convolution. The kernels you invented are the classic blur, sharpen, and Sobel filters used everywhere in computer vision.
