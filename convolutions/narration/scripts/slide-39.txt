Here's a bonus challenge, and it's a fun one. What happens when you apply the same kernel over and over? Try Kernel B ten times and look at the results after one, three, five, and ten passes. Then run Kernel A twenty times. Make a guess first, then see if you're right.

The big question comes at the end. Real neural networks stack many convolution layers. Based on what you've just seen, what do you think the early layers detect? What about the middle layers, and the deeper ones? Write down your own thoughts.
