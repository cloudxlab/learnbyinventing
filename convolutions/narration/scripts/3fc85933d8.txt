# ON SCREEN:
# Design challenge: Invent your own kernel! Can you create: A stronger blur? (Try 5 5 instead of 3 3) An emboss effect? (Hint: light from one direction, shadow on the other) A kernel that detects only corners?
# ---
Here's a fun one: now *you* get to be the designer. Try a stronger blur first, a five-by-five kernel instead of three-by-three. Then see if you can make an emboss, where light hits from one direction and shadow falls on the other. Finally, take a shot at a kernel that only picks out corners. Experiment, and see what you discover.
