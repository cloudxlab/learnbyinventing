# ON SCREEN:
# Quick Check: Why does bilinear upsampling produce smoother results than nearest-neighbor? A. It uses more memory B. It blends neighboring pixels instead of copying one C. It makes the image brighter D. It adds random noise for smoothness
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: B. Bilinear interpolation creates gradual transitions by blending four neighbors. Nearest-neighbor just copies one pixel, creating hard edges.
# ---
That's right, it's B! Bilinear upsampling blends the four nearest pixels, so the colors shift gradually instead of jumping. Nearest-neighbor just copies one pixel, which is why you get those blocky, hard edges.
