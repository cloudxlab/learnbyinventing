# ON SCREEN:
# Before coding think on paper. You have two adjacent pixels with values 100 and 200 . You need to insert a new pixel between them. What value would look most natural?
# [Fragment 1]: You re doing interpolation filling in values between known points. 100 125 150 175 200 creates smooth, even spacing.
# ---
That's interpolation: filling in values between two points you already know. Notice how 125, 150, and 175 step up evenly from 100 to 200, so the new pixel blends in smoothly. Hold onto that idea, because it's exactly what you'll need when we start stretching images.
