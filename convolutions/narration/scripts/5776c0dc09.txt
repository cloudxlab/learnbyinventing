# ON SCREEN:
# What You Just Invented
# ---
Look at what you just did. You started with a blurry photo and a question: how do we find edges? Then you slid a tiny grid of numbers across the image, multiplied, and added. That's a convolution, and you built it from scratch.

You found that changing the numbers in that grid changes what it detects. And then you realized a network could learn those numbers on its own. That's a convolutional neural network, and you invented the core idea yourself.
