Now we're moving into downsampling, where a 100 by 100 image has to become 50 by 50. That's four times fewer pixels, so every output pixel has to come from somewhere in the input.

Before you write any code, grab a pencil. Which input pixels feed each output pixel? What single value could you give it? Try to come up with at least three choices. And do we lose information along the way?
