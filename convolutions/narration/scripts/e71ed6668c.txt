Here's a challenge for you. Can you write a function that slides a small three-by-three window across a grayscale image? The surprising part is that this one function can give you edge maps, blurs, and sharpening. All you change is the numbers inside the window. If you already see how to build it, go ahead and skip to the end. Otherwise, let's invent it together, one small step at a time.
