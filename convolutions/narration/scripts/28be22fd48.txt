Skipping pixels is fast, but it throws away information. Whatever those skipped pixels were showing, we just lose it. So let's think about a smarter way to shrink an image. What if, instead of ignoring pixels, every small block of four pixels got blended into one? Try to picture how you'd do that, then let's see.
