Here's a challenge: turn a color photo into black and white. Think about what a pixel really is: three numbers, red, green, and blue. How could you squash those three into just one brightness value? Try the simplest idea first, giving each channel an equal vote.

Before you code, think about what could go wrong. These values are small whole numbers, and adding three of them might overflow. Give it a shot on your own, and check the hint if you get stuck.
