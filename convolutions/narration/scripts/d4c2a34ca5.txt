Time to try your first conversion yourself. Colour images store red, green, and blue for every pixel, and grayscale needs just one number. So how would you combine those three into one? Start with the simplest idea: treat all three channels equally. Give it a go before you peek at the hint. One thing to watch for is that pixel values are small integers, so adding them can overflow.
