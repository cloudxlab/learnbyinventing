# The most powerful operation in computer vision is surprisingly simple: slide a small grid of weights over the image, and at each position compute a weighted sum .
# ---
Here's the big idea. The most powerful operation in computer vision is surprisingly simple. You take a small grid of numbers, called weights, and slide it across the image. At every spot, you multiply each weight by the pixel beneath it and add everything up. That's it. Keep that picture in your head as we go.
