# ON SCREEN:
# The most powerful operation in computer vision is surprisingly simple: slide a small grid of weights over the image, and at each position compute a weighted sum .
# ---
Here's the big idea of this whole chapter, and it's surprisingly simple. Take a small grid of weights, and slide it across the image. At every position, multiply each weight by the pixel underneath it, and add everything up. That's one weighted sum, and it gives you one new number. Repeat it everywhere, and you've built the most powerful tool in computer vision.
