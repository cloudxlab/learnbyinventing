# [Fragment 0]: Sharpening. The center has weight 5 and the neighbors have 1. This boosts the center pixel relative to its neighbors the opposite of blurring. Edges get crisper, details pop.
# ---
Sharpening does the opposite of blurring. The center pixel gets a big weight of five while its neighbors only get one, so the center stands out more against its surroundings. That's why edges look crisper and small details pop.
