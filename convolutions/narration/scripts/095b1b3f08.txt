Now it's your turn to build something. Here's a small but useful tool: a function that surrounds an image with a border of zeros. Think about why we'd want that. When a filter slides over the image, the edges get awkward, and a ring of zeros gives it room to work.

Before you code, picture the new image. How much bigger is it than the original? Where does the original sit inside it? Try it yourself first, and peek at the hint only if you get stuck.
