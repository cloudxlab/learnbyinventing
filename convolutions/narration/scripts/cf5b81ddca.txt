# [Fragment 0]: Idea: For each pixel, subtract its left neighbor from its right neighbor. Where the image is flat, the difference is near zero. At an edge, it s large. [code block] The big numbers (150) mark the edge!

Here's the idea: for each pixel, subtract its left neighbor from its right neighbor. In the flat areas you get nearly zero, but right at the edge the jump shows up as a big number, 150. You just built an edge detector out of simple subtraction.
