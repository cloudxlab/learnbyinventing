# ON SCREEN:
# Part 7: Neighborhood Operations
# ---
Up to now, we've been looking at one pixel at a time, asking what each one is on its own. But a single pixel doesn't tell you much. Is it part of an edge, a smooth patch, or just noise? To find out, you have to look at its neighbors. That's the big idea in this section: let's invent a way to let every pixel peek at the pixels around it.
