# To a computer, an image is just a grid of numbers: [code block]
# [Fragment 0]: Each pixel is three numbers: (Red, Green, Blue) . [code block]
# ---
Each pixel is really three numbers stacked together: how much red, how much green, and how much blue. Mix those three in different amounts and you get every color on your screen. So a color picture is just three grids of numbers layered on top of each other.
