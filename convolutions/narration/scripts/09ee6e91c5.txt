Nearest-neighbor upsampling gets the job done, but you've probably noticed it looks blocky. Each new pixel just copies its closest neighbor. We can do better by blending between neighbors, so the colors change smoothly. The building block is something called linear interpolation. Try writing it yourself before moving on.
