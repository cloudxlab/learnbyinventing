# ON SCREEN:
# Part 6: Finding Edges
# ---
Now we're moving into something really useful: finding edges. Think about what an edge actually is in a picture. It's the spot where the brightness suddenly jumps, like where a dark object meets a bright background. You already know how to slide a small grid across an image, so here's the question: could you design one that lights up only at those jumps? Let's find out.
