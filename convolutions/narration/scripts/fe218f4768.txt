# A color image has three layers stacked together: Red, Green, Blue. Write a function to extract just one channel:
# ---
Here's something new: a color image isn't just one grid of numbers. It's actually three grids stacked on top of each other, one for red, one for green, and one for blue. Mix them together and you get every color you see.

Now it's your turn. Write a function that pulls out just one of those layers. Give it a try before moving on.
