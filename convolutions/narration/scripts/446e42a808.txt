# Write bilinear upsampling:
# ---
Time to write your own bilinear upsampling. Think about what happens when you stretch a small image to a bigger one: the new pixels land between the old ones, so there's no value to simply copy. Each new pixel has to blend its four nearest neighbors, weighted by how close it sits to each. Sketch one example on paper first, then give it a try.
