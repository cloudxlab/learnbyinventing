# A color image has three layers stacked together: Red, Green, Blue. Write a function to extract just one channel:
# ---
Here's a fun one: a color image isn't just one grid of numbers. It's three grids stacked on top of each other, one for red, one for green, and one for blue. Mix them together and you get the full picture.

Now it's your turn. Try writing a function that pulls out just one of those channels. Give it a go before moving on.
