Now let's flip the problem around. You've got a 50 by 50 image, and you want a 100 by 100 one. Going smaller threw information away, but going bigger means making up pixels that were never there.

Before you write any code, grab a pencil. For each new pixel, which original pixel or pixels does it come from? And what if it lands right between two of them? Jot down two different strategies, then let's move on.
