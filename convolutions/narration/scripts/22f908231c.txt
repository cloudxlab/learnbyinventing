# ON SCREEN:
# Quick Check: Why does the standard formula give green the largest weight (0.587)? A. Green light has the most energy B. The human eye is most sensitive to green C. There are more green pixels in most images D. It s arbitrary any weights work equally well
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: B. Human vision has peak sensitivity in the green range, so green contributes more to perceived brightness.
# ---
That's right, it's B. Our eyes are most sensitive to green light, so green naturally contributes the most to how bright a pixel looks to us. That's why the formula weights it so heavily.
