# ON SCREEN:
# Before coding think first. A pixel has R=200, G=50, B=50. You need a single gray value. Should it be closer to 200 (because red is high) or closer to 100 (the average of all three)?
# ---
Here's a quick puzzle before any code. Imagine a pixel with a lot of red, and only a little green and blue. We need one gray number to stand in for all three. Should it lean toward the red, up near 200? Or should it land around 100, the plain average? Take a moment and decide on your own, and be ready to explain why. There's no wrong guess here, only a guess you can test.
