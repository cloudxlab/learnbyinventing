# ON SCREEN:
# Before coding think first. A pixel has R=200, G=50, B=50. You need a single gray value. Should it be closer to 200 (because red is high) or closer to 100 (the average of all three)?
# ---
Here's a quick puzzle before we write any code. Imagine a pixel with a lot of red, and only a little green and blue. We need to squeeze those three numbers into one gray value. So what should that gray be? Should it sit close to 200, because red is so strong? Or closer to 100, the plain average of all three? Take a moment, grab a pencil, and make a guess. There's no wrong answer yet.
