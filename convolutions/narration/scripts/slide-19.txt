# ON SCREEN:
# Part 5: Growing an Image — Upsampling to 100x100
# ---
So far, we've been working with tiny images. Now let's go the other direction and grow one. Imagine taking a small picture and stretching it up to one hundred by one hundred pixels. The big question is: where do all those new pixels get their values from? Think about how you'd fill them in, and try it yourself before moving on.
