Here's a challenge that ties together everything you've learned so far. You're going to write a function that slides a grid of weights across the whole image. At each spot, it combines the neighborhood of pixels under the grid into a single output number.

We'll use "valid" mode, so you only compute where the grid fits fully inside the image. That means the output comes out a little smaller than the input.
