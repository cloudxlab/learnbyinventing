# [Fragment 0]: Horizontal edge detector (Sobel-y). Negative weights on top, positive on bottom. It measures the brightness difference between top and bottom rows highlighting horizontal edges.
# ---
This one looks for horizontal edges. The negative weights sit on top and the positive ones on the bottom, so it compares the brightness above a spot with the brightness below it. Where those two differ a lot, you've found a horizontal edge.
