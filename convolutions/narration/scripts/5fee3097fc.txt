# ON SCREEN:
# Skipping pixels throws away information. A better approach: average each 2 2 block into one pixel.
# ---
Skipping pixels was a quick way to shrink an image, but it threw away a lot of detail. Whatever was in those skipped pixels is simply gone. So let's think about something smarter. What if, instead of ignoring pixels, we used all of them? Picture a small two-by-two block of pixels, and squash it down into a single pixel by averaging them.
