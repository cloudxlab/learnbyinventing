The answer is B. This filter detects horizontal edges. The top row is all minus ones and the bottom row is all plus ones. In a smooth area, the top and bottom nearly cancel out and you get close to zero. But at a horizontal edge where brightness changes sharply from top to bottom, the filter produces a large value. That's how it finds edges.
