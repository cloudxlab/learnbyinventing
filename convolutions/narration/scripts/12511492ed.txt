Here's the catch with sliding a kernel over an image: it shrinks the output. A hundred by hundred image becomes ninety-eight by ninety-eight. If we want to keep the original size, we pad the border with zeros first, so the kernel has room to sit on the edge pixels. Try picturing where those extra zeros go.
