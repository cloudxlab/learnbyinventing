# Part 2: The `?` Wildcard New rule: in the pattern, matches exactly one character -- any character. [code block]
# ---
Now we've got exact matching working, but real patterns need flexibility. So here's a new rule: a question mark in the pattern stands for exactly one character, and it doesn't care which one. Take a look at the example and think about how your matcher would have to change. Try working it out yourself before we move on.
