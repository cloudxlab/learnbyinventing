Every time you search for a file, check an email address on a signup form, or grep through a server log, a pattern matcher is quietly doing the work. In this chapter, you're going to invent one yourself. We'll start with exact matching, then add wildcards, and use the recursion you already know to make them powerful. Finally, you'll meet Python's own regular expression tool and use it on a real web-server log.
