Look at how far you've come. You started by just comparing two strings character by character, and you ended up building a real glob matcher, the same kind of thing your shell uses when you type a star to match files.

The big insight was the wildcard. You didn't try to guess how much to swallow, you just tried both options and let recursion sort it out. Then regular expressions gave you a richer language, and you used it to mine real data from a server log.
