You just invented something big. That trick in your matcher, where you try both options and succeed if either one works, is called backtracking. It's the same idea behind solving mazes, Sudoku, and N-Queens puzzles. It's also how simple regex engines work under the hood. So the next time you write a pattern, you'll know what's happening inside. Nicely done.
