Let's start with the simplest version of matching: do these two strings match exactly? But here's the twist. You can't just compare the whole strings. You have to go one character at a time. Why? Because soon some pattern characters will stop meaning themselves, and whole-string comparison won't work anymore. Think recursively: if the first characters match, and the rest match too, you've got a match. Before you code, ask yourself what the base cases are. Give it a try.
