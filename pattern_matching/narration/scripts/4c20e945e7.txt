That branching trick you just built, where you try both options and succeed if either one works, has a name: backtracking. It's the same idea behind solving mazes, Sudoku, and the N-Queens puzzle. It's also how simple regex engines work under the hood. You started with a wildcard matcher and ended up inventing a technique that shows up all over computer science.
