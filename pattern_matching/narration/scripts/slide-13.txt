Your date pattern works, but it's a bit too forgiving. It would happily accept a month of ninety-nine. So let's tighten it up. Start with the day. Can you split the range one to thirty-one into a few alternatives? One group covers the early days, another covers the tens and twenties. Then ask yourself what covers thirty and thirty-one. Months work the same way. Once you've got your pieces, group them in parentheses and join them with the "or" symbol. Give it a go before moving on. And don't worry about February thirtieth.
