# Match Dates Dates appear as or (day, month, year -- separator is or ). Write a regex string so that finds dates. Start simple: two digits, a separator, two digits, a separator, four digits. [code block]

Dates show up in all sorts of formats, and that's what makes them a great regex challenge. Here, the day, month, and year can be separated by a dash or a slash. Let's start simple, and just match two digits, a separator, two digits, another separator, and then four digits. Think about which pattern pieces stand for "a digit" and "one of these characters," then try writing it yourself before moving on.
