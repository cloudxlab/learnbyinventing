# Part 2: The `?` Wildcard New rule: in the pattern, matches exactly one character -- any character. [code block]
# ---
Now we're adding a new rule: a question mark in the pattern can stand in for exactly one character, and it doesn't matter which one. So a pattern like c-question-mark-t would match cat, cot, or cut. Take a look at the code, and think about how your exact-match function would need to change. What would you do differently when you hit one of these special characters?
