Time to put regular expressions to work on something real: dates. You've probably seen them written a few ways, like day, month, year, with a dash or a slash between the parts. Let's start simple. Try writing a pattern for two digits, a separator, two digits, another separator, then four digits. Take a shot at it yourself before you look at anything else.
