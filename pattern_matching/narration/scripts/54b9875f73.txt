Your simple date pattern happily matches ninety-nine slash eighty-eight -- clearly not a real date. Now tighten it up. Use alternatives with the pipe symbol to limit days to one through thirty-one and months to one through twelve. Group your alternatives in parentheses. Don't worry about impossible dates like February thirtieth -- even professional validators leave that to code outside the regex.
