# Part 3: The `*` Wildcard The big one: matches zero or more characters -- any characters. [code block]

Part three is the big one: the star wildcard. Unlike the question mark, which stands in for exactly one character, this one can match zero characters, one, or a whole long stretch of anything.

Before you look at the code, think about it. What makes this harder than the question mark? Try to picture where things could go wrong.
