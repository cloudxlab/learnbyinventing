Here's a fun security task. Imagine you're auditing a pile of text, and you need to catch anything that looks like a credit card number leaking out. Those numbers come in four groups of four digits, and people separate them with spaces, dashes, or nothing at all.

Think about the pattern before you write it. What's the building block that keeps repeating, and how many times does it repeat? The hint points you toward that. Give it a real try, then test it against the examples.
