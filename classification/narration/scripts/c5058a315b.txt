# Part 2: Making Big Bigger
# ---
Welcome to Part 2. So far we've seen the problem: when the numbers get big, our model gets stuck, and the signal we care about gets squashed. Now let's flip that around. How could we take values that are already big and make them even bigger, so the differences really stand out? Think about what kind of operation stretches the gap between a large number and a small one. Hold that thought, and let's explore it together.
