# What You Just Invented
# ---
You've covered a lot of ground to get here, and you built every piece of it yourself. You started by asking how to score a guess, and you ended up with the same tools experts use for classification. You found a way to squash any number into a probability. You invented a loss that punishes confident mistakes. You also worked out how to nudge the weights downhill. Take a moment to match each of your home-grown ideas to its official name.
