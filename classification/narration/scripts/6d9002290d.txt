You've built cross-entropy as a way to score a classifier, and you've seen gradient descent nudge weights downhill. Now let's put the two together. Cross-entropy will be our loss, and gradient descent will be our engine. Before you scroll ahead, think about what we'll need to compute, and how the loss tells each weight which way to move.
