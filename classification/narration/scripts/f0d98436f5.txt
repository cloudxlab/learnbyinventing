Welcome to Part 3: Softmax. So far, you've been building ways to get a model to score its options. Now we'll face a new puzzle: what if there are more than two classes, and those raw scores can be any numbers at all, even negative ones? Our job is to turn them into something that behaves like real probabilities. Let's see if you can invent it.
