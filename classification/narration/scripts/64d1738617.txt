# ON SCREEN:
# Imagine you have two numbers: 1 and 9 . How would you turn them into fractions that add up to 1?
# [Fragment 0]: Simple divide each by their total: [math]
# ---
Just divide each number by the total. One divided by ten, and nine divided by ten, gives you point one and point nine, and those two fractions always add up to one, no matter what numbers you start with.
