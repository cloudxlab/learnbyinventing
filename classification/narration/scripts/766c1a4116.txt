# ON SCREEN:
# Imagine you have two numbers: 1 and 9 . How would you turn them into fractions that add up to 1?
# ---
Here's a little puzzle to warm up. Say you've got two numbers, one and nine. Can you turn them into fractions that add up to exactly one? Pause here and think about it. What would you do to each number? Try it on paper first, and see what you come up with.
