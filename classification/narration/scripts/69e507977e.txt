# If inputs can be negative (and they often are in ML): [math] a negative number becomes positive and big. That s wrong a small (negative) value should stay small. But [math] small and positive.
# [Fragment 0]: The exponential [math] has a perfect property: Large positive [math] very large output Zero output is 1 Negative [math] output is small but always positive
# ---
The exponential function has just the behavior we need. Big positive inputs give huge outputs, zero gives exactly one, and negative inputs give tiny values that stay positive. So a small negative number stays small instead of flipping into something big.
