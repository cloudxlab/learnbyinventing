Here are five reflection questions. Take a moment to answer each one in your own words. First, how would you explain what a logarithm is to a friend, without using the word logarithm? Second, why does log of 1 equal zero for every base? Third, could you use the same binary search guessing strategy to find a square root? Fourth, when would you need a log in a base other than 10? And fifth, if a computer has 16 bits of memory, how many different values can it store?
