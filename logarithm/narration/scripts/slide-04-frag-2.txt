# ON SCREEN:
# So when someone asks what is log(1000)? they are really asking: 10 to the power of what gives 1000? What is the answer?
# [Fragment 2]: ? 1
# ---
Three zeros means ten gets multiplied by itself three times. Think about it: ten times ten is a hundred, and one more ten makes a thousand. So the answer is three, and that's what a logarithm tells us.
