# ON SCREEN:
# So when someone asks what is log(1000)? they are really asking: 10 to the power of what gives 1000? What is the answer?
# [Fragment 4]: ? 3
# ---
Three! Ten times ten times ten is a thousand, so the power we were hunting for is three. That's all a logarithm is: the answer to "how many tens do I multiply together?"
