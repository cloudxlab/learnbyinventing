# What is log 2 (6)? In other words: 2 to the power of what gives 6?
# ---
Here's a new puzzle for you. What is log base 2 of 6? Put another way, 2 to the power of what gives you 6? Take a moment and think about it. Two squared is four, and two cubed is eight, so where does six land? Make a guess before we go on. Pencil and paper are welcome.
