# Every guess eliminates half of the remaining range. After 1 guess: half the range left After 5 guesses: 1/32 of the range After 10 guesses: 1/1024 of the range After 20 guesses: 1/1,048,576 of the range
# ---
Look at how fast the range shrinks. Every single guess cuts what's left in half. After five guesses, you're down to just one thirty-second of the range. After ten, about one in a thousand. And after twenty guesses, you've narrowed things to roughly one in a million. That's incredible power for so few questions.
