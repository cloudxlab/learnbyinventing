# [Fragment 4]: The power tells you how many zeros. The number of zeros tells you the power. Going from power number is exponentiation . Going from number power is logarithm .
# ---
Here's the big idea: powers and zeros are two sides of the same coin. If you know the power, you know how many zeros. If you count the zeros, you know the power. Going from the power to the number is exponentiation, and going backwards, from the number to the power, has its own name: the logarithm.
