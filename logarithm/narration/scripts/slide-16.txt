Here's the thing about guessing: every single guess throws away half of what's left. One guess, and half the range is gone. Five guesses, and you're down to a thirty-second of where you started. Ten guesses, about one part in a thousand.

Think about how fast that shrinks. Take a moment to picture it, because the next number is the surprising one.
