# Quick Check: Does K-Means always converge (stop changing)? A. Yes each iteration can only decrease or maintain the total distance; it must eventually stabilize B. No it can oscillate forever C. Only if k = 2 D. Only for 2D data
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: A. K-Means provably converges. Each step either reduces the total distance or leaves it unchanged. Since there are finitely many possible assignments, it must terminate.
# ---
That's right, it's A. Every step either shrinks the total distance or leaves it the same, and since there are only so many ways to assign points to clusters, K-Means can't keep improving forever. It has to settle down eventually, and that's a real guarantee.
