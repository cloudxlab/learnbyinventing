We've covered a lot, so let's pull it together. You started with a pile of unlabeled points and a simple question: how do you find groups without anyone telling you what they are? Along the way, you invented the whole loop yourself. Pick some centers, assign every point to its nearest one, then move each center to the middle of its group. Repeat until nothing changes. Take a moment to look at this recap, and notice how each piece you built fits into that one idea.
