# Assign Points to Nearest Center You have 10 points and 2 centers. For each point, figure out which center is closer, and put that point in that center's group. Write that returns a list of groups — one group per center. Each group is a list of points assigned to that center.
# ---
Here's a fun one: you've got ten points scattered around and two centers. Your job is to take each point and decide which center it's closer to. Then drop it into that center's group. When you're done, you'll have one list of points per center. Pause here and try writing this yourself. You already know how to measure distance, so think about how you'd compare the two.
