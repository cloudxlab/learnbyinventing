You've just invented K-Means, piece by piece. Look at what you did: you measured distances, which is Euclidean distance. You assigned points to their nearest center, then averaged each group to move the centers. Repeating those two steps until nothing changed is convergence. Adding up distances gave you inertia, and plotting it against K gave you the elbow method. Nicely done.
