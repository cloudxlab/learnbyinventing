# Part 1: Grouping Points by Distance Here's a bunch of points scattered on a 2D plane. Your goal: split them into two groups so that points in the same group are close together. How would you start? You need some notion of a "center" for each group. The simplest idea: just pick two points and call them the centers. Then assign every point to whichever center is closer.

Here's a bunch of points scattered across a plane, and your job is to split them into two groups, so that points in each group sit close together. How would you even begin? Think about it for a moment. Each group needs some kind of center. The simplest idea is to pick two of the points and call them your centers. Then every other point just joins whichever center is closer.
