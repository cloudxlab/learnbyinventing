Here's a challenge for you, and it's a big one. Can you write a function that takes any set of points and automatically sorts them into clusters? Start by picking random centers. Then repeat two moves: assign each point to its nearest center, and move each center to the average of its group. Keep going until the centers stop moving. Give it a real try before you look at anything else. If you've already got it, feel free to skip ahead to the end.
