You just built the whole K-Means algorithm, piece by piece, and now it's time to meet its real names. Your "nearest center" step is the assignment step, built on Euclidean distance. Your group averages are the update step, where centroids get recomputed. Repeating until nothing changes is Lloyd's algorithm, from 1957. Take a second and notice how much you invented yourself.
