# ON SCREEN:
# Write the straight-line (Euclidean) distance between two points:
# [Fragment 0]: Hint: square the differences, sum them, take the square root. [math]
# ---
Here's a hint if you're stuck. Take the difference in each coordinate and square it, add those squares together, then take the square root of the total. That gives you the straight-line distance between the two points.
