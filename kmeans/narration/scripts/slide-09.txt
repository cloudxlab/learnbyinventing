Congratulations, you just invented K-Means clustering! Look at what you built. You picked starting centers, assigned every point to its nearest one, moved each center to the average of its group, and repeated until nothing changed. Those steps already have names: initialization, assignment, update, and convergence. And notice that nobody gave you labels. You found the groups using only distances and averages. That's unsupervised learning.
