# Part 2: Iterate Until Done You can now assign points to centers and compute better centers from the groups. What happens if you keep doing this — assign, recompute, assign, recompute?
# ---
Here's a question worth pausing on. You can already assign every point to its nearest center, and you can move each center to the middle of its group. So what happens if you just keep going? Assign, recompute, assign, recompute. Take a moment and make a guess. Do the centers keep wandering forever, or do they settle somewhere?
