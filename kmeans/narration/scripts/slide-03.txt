# Assign Points to Nearest Center You have 10 points and 2 centers. For each point, figure out which center is closer, and put that point in that center's group. Write that returns a list of groups — one group per center. Each group is a list of points assigned to that center.
# ---
Here's a fun little challenge. You've got ten points scattered around and two centers. Your job is to go through each point and ask, which center is it closer to? Then drop it into that center's group.

When you're done, you'll have a list of groups, one for each center. Think about how you'd measure closeness first. Give it a real try before moving on.
