# ---
Let's start with a simple-sounding problem. Imagine a bunch of points scattered across a flat plane, and your job is to split them into two groups, where points in the same group sit close together. How would you begin? Think about it for a moment. Each group needs some kind of center. The simplest idea is to pick two points and call them your centers, then send every other point to whichever center is nearer. Let's see how that goes.
