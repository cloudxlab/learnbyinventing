Now that you can build a tree, let's use it. Your next job is to write a find function that tells us whether a value is in the tree.

Here's the neat part. At every node, you compare once, then you get to throw away half the tree. Does that remind you of anything? It's the same halving trick you invented in binary search. Give it a try yourself before moving on.
