Here's a secret: recursion isn't magic. Every time Python calls a function, it quietly stacks up where it left off, and that's all the call stack is. So let's prove it. Can you do in-order traversal with no recursion at all, using a plain list as your own stack? Walk left as far as you can, pushing nodes. Then pop one, that's your next value, and step right. Try it yourself first.
