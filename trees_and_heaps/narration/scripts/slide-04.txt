Now that we know *why* we want a tree, let's make one searchable. There's just one rule to remember: everything in a node's left subtree is smaller than that node, and everything in its right subtree is larger.

Before you write any code, open the visualization and play with it. Insert ten, twelve, one, eleven, thirteen, and three, one at a time. Watch where each number lands, and ask yourself why it went left or right.
