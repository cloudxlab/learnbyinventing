Your tree is a heap, and the shape rule says new items go at the very end of the list. So let's add the new value there. That keeps the tree tidy, but there's a catch. The newcomer might be smaller than its parent, and that breaks the heap rule. Before you look at any hints, think about how you'd repair it. Where would you move the new value, and how far could it travel? Try writing the push function yourself. Then think about the worst case. How many swaps could one push ever cost?
