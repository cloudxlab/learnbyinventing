Here's the catch with what we just built. Try inserting the numbers one through ten in order, then print the tree. What shape do you get? Every single insert goes to the right, so your "tree" is really just a chain, a glorified linked list. Finding something is slow again. Sorted input is the worst case for a plain binary search tree. Self-balancing trees fix this by rotating nodes as they go. Try the visualization and watch one stay shallow.
