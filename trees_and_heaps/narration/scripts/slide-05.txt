Time to write your first piece of the binary search tree: insert. The idea is to think recursively. When you reach an empty spot, that's where the new value goes. Otherwise, ask yourself whether the value is smaller or larger than the current node, and which side that points to. Insert it into that subtree, then reattach the result.
