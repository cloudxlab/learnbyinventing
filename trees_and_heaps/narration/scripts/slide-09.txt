Now let's flip it around. Can you write the mirror image, a function that finds the largest value? The rule's the same: no comparing values, just walk. Think about which direction the bigger numbers live, and follow it. Give it a try yourself first. And notice that both of these take only about as many steps as the tree is tall. On a balanced tree with a million values, that's roughly twenty steps.
