# ON SCREEN:
# Reflection questions about BSTs, heaps, balance, index arithmetic, heap sort, and data structure trade-offs
# ---
Take a moment with these questions. First, compare the rules of a BST and a heap. How does that one difference change what each is good at? Second, why does sorted input wreck a plain BST, and what fixes it? Third, why can a heap live in a plain list with no node objects? Fourth, how does heap sort compare to the sorts you built before? And finally, give a real example where a heap beats a BST, and one where a BST wins. Write your answers in your own words.
