Sorted input is the worst input for a plain BST. Self-balancing trees (AVL, Red-Black) fix this by rotating nodes on insert to keep the height at log n. Here's a puzzle. Insert the numbers one through ten in sorted order, then print the tree. What shape do you get? Try it yourself before moving on. Every insert goes right, so the tree is really just a chain, a glorified linked list, and find slows back down to checking every node. Sorted input is the worst case for a plain tree. The fix is self-balancing trees, which rotate nodes as you insert to keep the height small. Play with the visualization and watch one stay shallow.
