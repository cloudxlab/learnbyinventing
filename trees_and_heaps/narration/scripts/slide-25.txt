You just climbed a ladder of trade-offs, and it's worth looking back at the view. A dictionary promises the most, instant lookup of any key, but it pays in memory and loses ordering. A BST gives you total order, and costs you a little work per operation, plus a balance headache. A heap promises only the minimum, and that's exactly why it's so simple and fast.

So choosing a data structure just means paying only for the promises you actually use. And look what else you picked up: recursion as a stack you manage yourself, and turning plain text into a tree. Well done.
