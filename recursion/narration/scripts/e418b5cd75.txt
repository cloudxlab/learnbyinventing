Here's a fun one: how many times does 5 fit into 17, and what's left over? Before you write any code, try it by hand. Keep subtracting 5 from 17 and count how many times you can do it before what's left is smaller than 5. What's the remainder? Then think about the edge case: what if the starting number is already smaller than the divisor? And after just one subtraction, how does the new problem relate to the original?
