You've just built a lot, so let's take a moment to look back. You started with a simple idea: a function that calls itself on a smaller version of the same problem. Then you used it for factorial, powers, Euclid's algorithm, Hanoi, and Fibonacci. Now ask yourself what pattern kept showing up, and why it worked.
