Here's a puzzle for you. Say you want the factorial of some number, and you already know how to get the factorial of the number just below it. How would that help?

Try writing the factorial as the number times a smaller factorial, and keep shrinking until you hit something you know instantly, with no multiplication at all. That's your base case. Everything else is the recursive case.

Grab a pencil and work it out first.
