# Try it yourself! Move all discs from peg A to peg C. Rules: one disc at a time, never place a larger disc on a smaller one. Start with 1 or 2 discs to discover the pattern, then try 3.
# ---
Now it's your turn to play! Your goal is to move every disc from peg A over to peg C. You can only move one disc at a time, and you can never put a bigger disc on top of a smaller one. Don't jump straight to three discs. Start with just one, then try two, and notice what you're doing each time. See if a pattern starts to show up. Then give three a shot. Take your time with this one.
