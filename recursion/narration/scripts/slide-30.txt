Here's a fun one: the Fibonacci sequence. Each number is just the sum of the two before it, so it's a natural fit for recursion, with two recursive calls, just like Tower of Hanoi. Try writing it yourself and test it on a small input. Then time it on a bigger one, and watch what happens. Something's off, and your job is to figure out why. Once you see the problem, you'll invent a clever fix: a memory that remembers answers so you never repeat work.
