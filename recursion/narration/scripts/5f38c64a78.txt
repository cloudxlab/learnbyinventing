Here's the full list of moves for two discs: small to B, big to C, small onto the big one at C. Now look at the pattern for any number of discs. You move everything above the biggest disc out of the way, move the biggest disc across, then move that whole pile on top of it. Moving those smaller piles is just the same puzzle again, so you'll need two recursive calls.
