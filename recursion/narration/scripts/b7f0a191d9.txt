Here's a challenge for you, and it's a famous one: the Tower of Hanoi. You've got a stack of disks on one peg, and you need to move them all to another peg, one at a time, never putting a bigger disk on a smaller one. Write a function that returns the list of moves, using recursion only, with two recursive calls. If you feel ready, give it a real shot. If not, no worries.
