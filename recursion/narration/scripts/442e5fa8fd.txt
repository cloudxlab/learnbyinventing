# ON SCREEN:
# Part 6: Tower of Hanoi Double Recursion
# ---
Now for something more fun: the Tower of Hanoi. Picture three pegs and a stack of disks, biggest on the bottom. You can only move one disk at a time, and you can never put a bigger disk on a smaller one.

Here's what makes it special. Every recursive function you've written so far called itself once. This one will call itself twice. Before we look at any code, grab a pencil and try a tiny tower of two or three disks by hand. Notice what you do.
