Here's a pattern worth pausing on. We're counting the moves for the Tower of Hanoi. With one disk, it takes one move. Two disks take three. Three disks take seven, and four disks take fifteen.

Look at those numbers for a moment. Can you spot what's going on? Try guessing the rule yourself, and then try to explain why it works.
