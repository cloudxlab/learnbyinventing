# Three pegs: A (source), B (spare), C (destination). A stack of discs on A, smallest on top. Before coding: for discs, work out the moves by hand: Move the top disc (small) somewhere safe Move the bottom disc (big) to C Move the small disc onto the big one at C
# ---
Time to try the Tower of Hanoi on your own. We've got three pegs: A is where the discs start, B is a spare, and C is where they need to end up. Start with just two discs, a small one stacked on a big one. Before you write any code, work it out by hand. Where can the small disc go to get out of the way? Once it's safe, what happens to the big one? Grab a pencil and try it.
