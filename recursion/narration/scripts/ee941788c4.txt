# The HCF (Highest Common Factor) of two numbers is the largest number that divides both of them evenly. It s also called the GCD (Greatest Common Divisor). Example: the factors of 12 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. The factors of 18 are 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18. The highest factor they share is 6 .
# ---
Let's try something new: finding the HCF of two numbers. That's the highest common factor, and some people call it the GCD. It's just the biggest number that divides both of them evenly.

Take 12 and 18. The numbers that divide 12 are one, two, three, four, six, and twelve. For 18, they're one, two, three, six, nine, and eighteen. The biggest one they share is six.
