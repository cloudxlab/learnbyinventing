Your job is to write a function that returns the list of moves needed to transfer the whole stack to another peg. You can only move one disc at a time, and you can never put a larger disc on top of a smaller one. If you already see how to do it, go ahead and skip to Part 7. Otherwise, give it a real try first, and we'll build it up together step by step.
