Here's a fun one: how many times does one number fit into another, and what's left over? Before you touch any code, try it by hand. Keep subtracting the divisor, count each subtraction, and stop when what's left is smaller than the divisor. Those two numbers are your quotient and remainder. Then ask yourself: what's the answer when there's nothing left to subtract? And after one subtraction, how does the new problem relate to the old one? Give it a go.
