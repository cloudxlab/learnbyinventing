Now it's your turn to invent. Here's the big idea: instead of figuring out the whole answer at once, try to express a problem in terms of a slightly smaller version of itself. Grab a pencil and write out the factorial of n, then see what's left over once you peel off the first number. Keep shrinking until you hit a case so simple you just know the answer. That's your base case. Everything else is your recursive case. Give it a real try before moving on.
