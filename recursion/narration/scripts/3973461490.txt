# A Second Route to Negative Exponents. power_climb uses x^n = x^(n+1) / x for negative n.
# ---
Here's an elegant alternative. Instead of flipping the sign and dividing at the end, you can make the recursion climb toward zero from below. For negative n, each call adds one to the exponent and divides by x, so minus three becomes minus two becomes minus one becomes zero, hitting the same base case. Write power_climb with one base case and two recursive branches. Hints are available if you want them.
