# Part 1: Factorial --- Your First Recursive Function
# ---
Welcome to your first recursive function! We're starting with something familiar: factorial. You probably know it as multiplying a number by every whole number below it, down to one. But here's the twist. Instead of using a loop, we're going to ask whether a problem can be solved by using a smaller version of itself. Let's see where that idea takes us.
