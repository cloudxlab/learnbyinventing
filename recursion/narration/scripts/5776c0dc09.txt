# ON SCREEN:
# What You Just Invented
# ---
You've now built something that took mathematicians and computer scientists a long time to name. Look at what you invented. A function that calls itself with a smaller problem. A base case that tells it when to stop. And a way of trusting the smaller answer to build the bigger one. You used that for factorial, powers, Euclid's method, Hanoi, and Fibonacci. Let's see how each of those maps to its standard name.
