Now that we've built the power function, let's put recursion to work on something new: finding the highest common factor of two numbers. That's the biggest number that divides both of them evenly. There's an ancient trick for this, called Euclid's method. Think about how you might shrink the problem step by step. You'll even get to reuse what you wrote in Part 5.
