Now let's try something with a bit more going on: the Fibonacci sequence. Each number is built from the two numbers just before it, so the rule itself points back at itself. That makes it a natural fit for recursion. Notice that, like Hanoi, each step needs two recursive calls instead of one. Before moving on, think about what the base cases should be.
