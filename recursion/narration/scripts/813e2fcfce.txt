# Part 7: Tower of Hanoi --- Two Recursive Calls
# ---
Now for a classic puzzle: the Tower of Hanoi. You've got three pegs and a stack of disks, and you need to move the whole stack to another peg. You can only move one disk at a time, and a bigger disk can never sit on a smaller one.

This one's different from everything so far, because the solution needs two recursive calls. Before you look at any code, grab a pencil and try it with just two or three disks. What do you notice?
