Here's a second way to handle negative exponents, and it's a neat one. Your first version probably flipped the answer at the end: compute the positive power, then take one over it. This time, skip the flip entirely.

Instead, let the recursion climb toward zero. When the exponent is negative, each call moves it up by one. So negative exponents reach the base case from below, just like positive ones reach it from above.
