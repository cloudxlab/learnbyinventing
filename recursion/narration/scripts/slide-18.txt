# Part 5: Division by Repeated Subtraction
# ---
We've been using recursion to multiply and to raise numbers to a power. Now let's try something a little trickier: division. Here's a question to think about. Division is really just asking, "how many times can I take one number away from another?" See if that idea sparks a way to solve it recursively. Take a moment to think about it before we dive in.
