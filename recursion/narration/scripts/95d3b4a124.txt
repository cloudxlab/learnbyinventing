# Quick Check: For discs, how many moves does Hanoi require? A. 8 B. 15 C. 16 D. 31
# [Fragment 0]: Answer: B. [math] moves.

Spot on, it's B, fifteen moves. Each extra disc doubles the work and adds one more move, so the count is two to the power of the number of discs, minus one. With four discs, that gives fifteen.
