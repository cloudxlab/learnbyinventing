Here's a new tool for your toolbox: XOR, short for "exclusive or." It works bit by bit. If the two bits are different, you get a one. If they match, you get a zero. Now for your challenge: write a function that takes three equal-length lists and combines them with XOR, element by element. Try working out a tiny example on paper first, then code it up.
