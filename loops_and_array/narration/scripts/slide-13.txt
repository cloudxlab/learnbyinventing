Mean absolute error is our next way of asking, "how wrong are my predictions, on average?" Before you code anything, think about each pair of actual and predicted values. What's the simplest way to measure how far off one guess is? And why might plain subtraction cause trouble when you add up lots of errors? Try the one-dimensional version yourself first. Then, if you're feeling brave, extend it to points with several coordinates.
