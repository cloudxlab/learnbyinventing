# Part 6: Probability and Sampling Random processes underlie stochastic gradient descent, Monte Carlo methods, and many more ML algorithms. You will build from a simple coin toss up to a temperature-scaled weighted sampler.

Welcome to Part 6: probability and sampling. Randomness is everywhere in machine learning. It's how we train on huge datasets one small piece at a time, and how we estimate answers by simulation. We'll start with something as simple as a coin toss, and build step by step to a sampler you can tune with a temperature dial.
