The answer is B, RMSE. RMSE squares each error before averaging, so the error of 20 becomes 400 — much bigger than the 1s. MAE just averages the absolute values: 1 plus 1 plus 1 plus 1 plus 20, divided by 5, gives 4.8. But RMSE equals the square root of 1 plus 1 plus 1 plus 1 plus 400, all divided by 5 — about 9.0. Squaring amplifies large errors, making RMSE much more sensitive to outliers.
