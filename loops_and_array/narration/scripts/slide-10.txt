# Part 2: Error Metrics How wrong is a model? These functions measure the gap between actual and predicted values.
# ---
Welcome to Part 2! Once you've built a model, the big question is: how wrong is it? Every prediction has a gap between what actually happened and what the model guessed. In this part, you'll invent ways to measure that gap, turning "pretty close" into a single number you can compare.
