Here's a new kind of question: given a pile of numbers and one target, which number in the pile is closest? Before you write any code, think it through on paper. If the target is seven and your list is two, nine, and eleven, how would you decide? Distance is just how far apart two numbers are, ignoring direction. Give it a go yourself first.
