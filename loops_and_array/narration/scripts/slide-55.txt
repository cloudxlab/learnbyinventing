# Circular Shift by k: the Triple-Reversal Trick Shifting left by could be done by calling k times — but that is O(n·k). There is a beautiful O(n) trick that uses reversal three times. Watch with : Reverse the first elements: Reverse the remaining elements: Reverse the whole array: — done! Write using this trick. You will want a helper that reverses just the slice between indices and in place.
# ---
Now for a fun one. Say you want to shift an array left by k positions. You could just shift by one, k times, but that gets slow when k is big. There's a much neater way: reverse the first part, reverse the rest, then reverse the whole thing. Before you write any code, try it by hand on a small list and watch the pieces land in place. Then build it yourself.
