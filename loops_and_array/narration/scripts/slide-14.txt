# RMSE vs MAE Your model's predictions for 5 data points have errors: [1, 1, 1, 1, 20]. Which error metric will be affected MORE by the single large error of 20? A. MAE (Mean Absolute Error) B. RMSE (Root Mean Square Error) C. Both are affected equally D. Neither — both ignore outliers
# ---
Here's a quick puzzle before we move on. Imagine your model made five predictions, and four of them were off by just one, but the fifth missed by twenty. That's one big miss in a sea of small ones.

Two metrics, MAE and RMSE, both summarize those errors. Which one do you think reacts more strongly to that single outlier? Take a moment and think about what each one does to the errors before averaging. Pick your answer, then we'll check it.
