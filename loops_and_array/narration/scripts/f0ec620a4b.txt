Here's a bonus section where we put loops to work in a few different ways. You'll use them to explore how a function behaves, to pull specific data out of a larger collection, and to capture patterns that show up again and again. These are optional, but they're a great way to sharpen your instincts. Take your time, try each one on your own, and notice which patterns start to feel familiar.
