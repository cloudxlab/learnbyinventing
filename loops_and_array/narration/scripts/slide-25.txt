Time to add two polynomials. Remember, we can store a polynomial as a simple list of coefficients. Adding them is just adding matching powers together. The tricky part is when the lists have different lengths. Line them up from the right, so the constant terms match, and pretend the missing higher terms are zero. Try writing it yourself before moving on.
