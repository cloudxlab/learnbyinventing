# ON SCREEN:
# Circular Shift by One Write that moves every element one position to the left, with the first element wrapping around to the end — in place, with only one temporary variable: becomes .
# ---
Here's a fun puzzle. Take a row of items and slide every one of them one spot to the left. The first item, which has nowhere to go, wraps around to the very end. Sounds simple, but here's the catch: you can only use one temporary variable, and you have to do it in place. Think about where that first element goes, and what has to happen before you can overwrite it. Give it a try on your own first.
