We're about to step into something that shows up all over machine learning: randomness. It sounds odd, but a lot of powerful algorithms, like stochastic gradient descent and Monte Carlo methods, depend on it. We'll start with something as simple as a coin toss. Then, step by step, we'll build up to a weighted sampler that you can tune with temperature. Ready?
