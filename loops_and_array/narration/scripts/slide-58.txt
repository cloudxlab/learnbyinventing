Here's a fun one: a formula you already know, but this time we'll explore it with a loop. Simple interest depends on a principal, a rate, and a time. We'll hold two of those fixed and let the third change, one step at a time.

Run the loop and watch the numbers as they come out. Do they grow by the same amount each time, or does the jump change? Then switch which value you hold fixed and try again. Is the pattern the same kind of growth, or something different?

Give it a go yourself before moving on. When you spot the pattern, try describing it in one sentence. There's a hint on the slide that connects it to a line you already know.
