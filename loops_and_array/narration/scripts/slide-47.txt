Here's a different kind of expression problem. This time, the expression isn't a string. It's stored as a nested list, where the first item is the operator and the next two are the left and right sides. And either side can be a number, or another list just like it.

Before you code, think about what the simplest case is. When do you stop? Then ask what to do with a list. Try it yourself first.
