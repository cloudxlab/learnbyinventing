Here's a puzzle: how would you multiply two polynomials when they're stored as plain lists of numbers? Think about how you'd do it on paper. You take one term at a time, multiply the whole other polynomial by it, then add up the pieces.

The twist is that a term's power decides how many zeros get tacked onto the end of its row. You already have the helpers you need, so try writing this one yourself first.
