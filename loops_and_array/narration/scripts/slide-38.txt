Here's a challenge that's all about experimenting. We've got a small array, and when we turn it into probabilities, the biggest value only gets about fifty-five percent. But we want the big values to really stand out and the small ones to shrink even more.

So before we normalize, how could we amplify the differences? Try multiplying everything by ten, then squaring, then cubing. Print the probabilities each time and ask yourself: did anything actually change, and why?

Then see if you can come up with some transformations of your own. Pause here and give it a real try.
