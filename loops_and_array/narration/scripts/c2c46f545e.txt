Here's a new tool for your toolbox, and it's a fun one. It's called XOR, short for exclusive or. Line up two numbers in binary, and for each bit, the result is one only when the two bits are different. Same bits give zero. Now try writing a function that takes three equal-length lists and combines them element by element. Give it a go yourself first.
