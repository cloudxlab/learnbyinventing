Here's a trick you'll use a lot: getting rid of one unknown at a time. Say you have two equations that both contain x. Scale them so the x terms match, subtract one from the other, and x simply disappears. What's left is a shorter equation with one fewer unknown.

The worked example walks through it. Then try the second one by hand before you code anything.
