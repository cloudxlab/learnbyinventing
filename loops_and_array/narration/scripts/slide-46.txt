Now let's try something that feels a little like Russian dolls: a list with lists tucked inside it, which might have more lists tucked inside those, as deep as you like. Your job is to flatten all of that into one simple list.

Think about what you do when you meet a number, and what you do when you meet another list. The second case is where recursion comes in. Give it a go yourself before looking at any hints.
