Now let's tackle a classic puzzle: shifting an array in a circle. Say you want to move everything left by k spots, with the front items wrapping around to the back. You could shift by one, k times, but that gets slow fast. There's a much slicker way, and it uses nothing but reversals. Watch how the three reversals play out, then try writing it yourself. Start with that helper that reverses just a slice, in place.
