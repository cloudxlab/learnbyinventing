# Part 3: Nearest Neighbor Finding the closest point is the core of the k-Nearest-Neighbors (k-NN) algorithm. We build up from 1-D to N-D, then add a pluggable distance function.

Welcome to Part 3, where we tackle a question you've probably asked a hundred times: which one is closest? Finding the nearest point is the heart of an algorithm called k-nearest neighbors. We'll start simple, with just one number, then grow to many dimensions, and finally make the distance measure something you can swap out.
