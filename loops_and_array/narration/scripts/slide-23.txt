Time to switch gears and talk about polynomials. You've probably seen expressions like three x squared plus two x plus five in math class. Here's the question: how would you store one in a program? A simple list of coefficients does the trick. We write them from the highest power down to the constant, so index zero holds the highest power's coefficient, and the last spot holds the plain number at the end. Let's see how we can put that to work.
