Now let's put that into practice. Your task is to write a function that takes a list of labels and gives back a single number: how mixed up that list is.

Think back to the marbles. A bag of all one color should score low, and a fifty-fifty bag should score high. Try working out a couple of small lists on paper first, then write your own version. Take your time with it. This is the heart of the whole tree.
