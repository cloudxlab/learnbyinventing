# Instead of splitting at a position , split at a threshold value : Left group: height threshold Right group: height threshold
# ---
Until now we've been splitting by position: sort the data, then cut after the third person or the fifth. But there's a more useful way to think about it. Instead of asking "where in the list do we cut?", ask "what height do we cut at?" Everyone below the threshold goes left, everyone at or above goes right. Let's see how that changes things.
