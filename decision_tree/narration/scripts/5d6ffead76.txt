Here's a challenge for you. Can you build a decision tree classifier completely from scratch? You'd take rows of numbers and labels, then recursively pick the split, a feature and a threshold, that leaves the groups as pure as possible. Stop when a group is pure, then walk each row down the tree. If that feels doable, go for it. Otherwise, the next slides will guide you step by step.
