Welcome! Picture a bag of marbles, all jumbled together, some red and some blue. Your job is to separate them, but how do you decide the best way to split the bag? Over this lesson, you'll invent a way to measure how mixed-up a group is. Then you'll use it to find the cleanest split, and discover that repeating the process builds a decision tree.
