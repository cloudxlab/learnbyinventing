# You have two bags of marbles red and blue: Bag Red Blue Total A 10 0 10 B 5 5 10 Which bag is more mixed up ?
# ---
Let's start with something simple. Imagine two bags of marbles, red and blue. Bag A has ten red marbles and nothing else. Bag B has five red and five blue. Take a moment and ask yourself: which bag is more mixed up? Go with your gut, and think about why.
