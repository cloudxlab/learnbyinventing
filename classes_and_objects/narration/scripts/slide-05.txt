Time for a fresh challenge. We're going to build a class called TwoPointLine, and it'll take two lists: one with the x values of two points, and one with their y values. From those, you'll work out the slope and the intercept, then store both on the object itself so they stick around. Then comes the second part: a method that takes a list of x values and gives back the predicted y for each one. You already know how to get a line through two points, so give it a go on your own first. Think about what belongs in the constructor and what belongs in the method.
