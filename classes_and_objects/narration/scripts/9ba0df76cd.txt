Welcome! So far, your programs have kept data, like lists and dictionaries, in one place and behavior, like functions, in another, and you've had to pass one to the other by hand. A class glues the two together. And here's why it matters for machine learning: a trained model is just an object, some learned numbers plus a function that uses them. By the end, you'll have invented the interface that scikit-learn and Keras use, built a decision tree from objects, and saved a model to disk.
