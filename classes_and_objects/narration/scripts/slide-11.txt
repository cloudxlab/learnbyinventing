Up to now, our objects have held simple things, like numbers and strings. But here's a neat idea: an object's attributes can be other objects. That's exactly how trees get built. Look at this company's expense tree. Each department is a node with its own expense, and up to two sub-departments hanging below it. Take a moment to read how the pieces connect.
