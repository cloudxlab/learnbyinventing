Here's a neat idea: you don't have to build everything from scratch. Look at Dog and Cat. They each get a brand-new method of their own, but they also inherit what Animal already knows. See that class name in parentheses? That's the parent. Everything the parent can do, the child can do too. So before you move on, ask yourself: what would you need to write for Dog, and what could you skip?
