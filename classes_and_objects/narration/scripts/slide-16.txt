Here's the big move. Instead of one giant chain of if-else statements about salaries, we're going to build the decision out of small objects. A DecisionNode holds a question and two children, and a Yes or No leaf holds a final answer. Before you code, think about how a node should pick which child to ask, and what the circumstance dictionary is for. Give it a try.
