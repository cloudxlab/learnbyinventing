# ---
Here's a neat trick. We've got an Animal class, and then Dog and Cat, each with their own special method. Look at the class line for Dog: the Animal sitting in parentheses is the parent. Dog and Cat don't redefine what Animal already does. They just inherit it. Everything the parent can do, the child can do too. Before we move on, think about why that saves us so much repeated code.
