# Part 5: A Decision Tree Is an Object You get a job offer. Do you take it? Your (very rational) rules: If the salary score is below 1 → No . Otherwise, if the learning opportunity score is below 1 → No . Otherwise, if the distance score is below 1 → Yes ; too far → No . That is a tree of decisions — and you can build it from objects.
# ---
Now for something more fun: a decision tree. Imagine you've got a job offer and you're deciding whether to take it. You've got a few simple rules. Is the salary good enough? Is there room to learn? Is the commute reasonable? Say no at any step, and the answer is no.

Take a moment to look at those rules. It's a tree of decisions, and we're going to build it out of objects. Can you already picture what the pieces might be?
