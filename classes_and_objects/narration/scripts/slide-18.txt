Your program built a useful tree, and then it exits, and poof, the whole thing is gone. Think about that for a second: a model you can't save is a model you have to retrain every single time. So let's fix that. Here's the catch: JSON only understands simple things like dicts, lists, numbers, and strings, not your custom objects. So our plan is to turn the object into a dict, write that to a file, and then rebuild the object on the way back.
