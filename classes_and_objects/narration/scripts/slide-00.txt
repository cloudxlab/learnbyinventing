Welcome! Up to now, your data and your functions have lived in separate boxes, and you've been passing one to the other by hand. In this chapter, we'll glue them together into something called an object. Why does that matter for machine learning? Because a trained model is exactly that: learned numbers plus a function that uses them. By the end, you'll have invented the interface that scikit-learn and Keras use, built a decision tree from objects, and saved a model to disk. Ready? Let's dive in.
